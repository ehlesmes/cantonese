Curriculum Index

A spiraled and integrated Cantonese learning curriculum. Select a chapter below to start reading.

Chapter 0

Pronunciation & Jyutping Fundamentals

Master the mechanical foundations of spoken Cantonese, including the six core tones, vowel length distinctions, silent stop consonants, and essential conversational exclamations.

Chapter 1

Greetings & Who You Are

Learn how to say hello, introduce yourself and others, make polite conversation, and ask basic identity questions using pronouns, equative verbs, and A-not-A question structures.

Chapter 2

Essential Purchases & Daily Slang

Learn how to buy items in a convenience store, ask for prices, count money in dollars, pay using cash or Octopus card, and use high-frequency loanwords and daily slang.

Chapter 3

Dining Out & Ordering

Step inside a local Hong Kong café (茶餐廳) to order authentic food and drinks, learn essential classifiers like cup and plate, express choices, and master the perfective completed aspect.

Chapter 4

Where Things Are: Presence & Places

Master the essential structures for describing where objects and places are in Cantonese. Learn to express presence and absence using existential verbs, locate items using the locative preposition, combine spatial directional markers, and convey natural obviousness using the particle lo.

Chapter 5

Time-Telling & Continuous Action

Master the structures for telling time, expressing schedules, and describing ongoing activities in Cantonese. Learn to ask and tell hours and minutes, use the unique five-minute interval system, apply the continuous aspect particle gan2, and integrate the popular slang hea.

Chapter 6

Ability, Permission & Experience

Master the modal verbs for expressing capability and permission in Cantonese. Understand the differences between ho2ji5, sik1, and wui5. Learn how to talk about past experiences using the aspect particle gwo3, and express what you can or cannot manage to do using the potential suffixes dak1 and m4dou2.

Chapter 7

Comparatives & Complex Descriptors

Master relative clauses and noun modifiers in Cantonese using the linking particle ge3. Learn how to make relative comparisons using the suffix di1 and form direct A-over-B comparative sentences using the marker gwo3. Explore superlatives with zeoi3.

Chapter 8

Advanced Particle Layering & Emotive Nuance

Master the soul of colloquial Cantonese by exploring how sentence-final particles shape emotion and meaning. Learn how to use ze1, maa3, wo3, and laa3, and see how layering them like laa3wo3 conveys subtle warnings and realizations.

Chapter 9

Split-Verbs & Native Idioms

Master the structural flexibility of Cantonese split-verbs like jau4seoi2 and coeng3go1. Learn how to parse and separate them with aspect markers, and discover colorful native idioms and expressions like ceoi1seoi2 and caau2jau4jyu2.

Chapter 10

Hypotheticals, Conditionals & Future Planning

Master conditional logic in spoken Cantonese using jyu4gwo2... zau6. Express hypothetical assumptions using gaa2si2, formulate future plans using daa2syun3 and hopes using hei1mong6, and explore local humor like faat3mung6.

Chapter 11

Narrative Cohesion & Complex Connectives

Master cohesive storytelling in colloquial Cantonese. Form complex connected paragraphs using advanced conjunctions such as because... therefore, although... but, not only... but also, even, since, and instead.

Chapter 12

Regrets, Wishes & Empathy

Master spoken structures to express past regrets using zou2zi1 and hau6fui3, make warm wishes using hei1mong6 and zuk1, and express deep empathy and comfort using caam2, zit6do6, sam1tung3, and fong3sam1.

Chapter 13

Passives, Focus & Indirect Speech

Master spoken passive structures using bei2, emphasize and clarify points using zik1hai6, and report statements or news using the verb waa6.

Chapter 14

Intensifiers, Exaggeration & Spoken Superlatives

Learn how to use Cantonese colloquial intensifiers to express extreme states, exaggerate emotions, and construct spoken superlative statements.

Chapter 15

Rhetorical Questions & Spoken Doubts

Master the art of forming rhetorical questions, expressing surprise or disbelief, and highlighting conversational skepticism using spoken Cantonese markers.

Chapter 16

Workplace Slang & Office Dynamics

Master the essential colloquial terms, slang, and expressions used in Hong Kong professional settings, from slacking off to getting a raise.

Chapter 17

Foodie Culture & Tea Restaurant Slang

Dive deep into the fast-paced foodie culture of Hong Kong. Learn how to customize your beverages like a local, master essential café shorthand, and use authentic slang for street-food hunting and late-night snacks.

Chapter 18

Shopping, Bargaining & Spoken Quantifiers

Chapter 19
