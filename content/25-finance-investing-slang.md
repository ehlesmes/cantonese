## 1. Stock Market & Trading Slang

Hong Kong has one of the world's most active financial markets, and trading
stocks is a popular national pastime. In daily conversation, talking about the
stock market uses highly expressive local slang:

- `炒股[caau2gu2|to speculate in stocks / trade stocks]`: Actively trading or
  speculating in the stock market (literally "frying stocks").
- `股票[gu2piu3|stock / share]`: A generic term for stocks or shares.
- `港股[gong2gu2|Hong Kong stocks / HK shares]`: Stocks traded on the Hong Kong
  Stock Exchange.
- `美股[mei5gu2|US stocks / US shares]`: Stocks traded on US stock exchanges.
- `散戶[saan2wu6|retail investor / small investor]`: Individual or retail
  investors, as opposed to large institutions (literally "scattered
  households").
- `大鱷[daai6ngok6|market shark / big player]`: Large institutional investors or
  hedge funds who manipulate market prices (literally "big crocodile").
- `開市[hoi1si5|market opens / market opening]`: The opening of the stock
  market.
- `收市[sau1si5|market closes / market closing]`: The closing of the stock
  market.
- `升[sing1|to rise / go up]`: When prices or indices go up.
- `跌[dit3|to fall / drop / decline]`: When prices or indices go down.
- `爆升[baau3sing1|to skyrocket / experience explosive growth]`: Sudden,
  massive, and rapid increases in stock price (literally "explosive rising").
- `牛市[ngau4si5|bull market]`: A rising bull market.
- `熊市[hung4si5|bear market]`: A declining bear market.
- `買入[maai5jap6|to buy in / purchase shares]`: Buying shares or entering a
  position.
- `賣出[maai6ceot1|to sell out / sell shares]`: Selling shares or exiting a
  position.
- `蟹[haai5|stuck / trapped in a losing stock (slang)]`: Being trapped in a
  losing stock position because the price fell after you bought it, making you
  unable to sell without a loss (literally "crab").
- `賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money]`: To make a profit or earn money from
  investments.
- `蝕[sit6|to lose money / suffer a loss / deficit]`: To lose money or suffer a
  financial deficit.

## 2. Personal Finance, Banking & Money Management

Beyond the fast-paced stock market, managing daily finances, banking, and wealth
involves standard and colloquial terms:

- `慳[haan1|to save (money/time) / cut down / economize]`: To save money, reduce
  expenses, or economize (highly frequent colloquial verb).
- `投資[tau4zi1|to invest / investment]`: To invest money, or refer to general
  investments.
- `定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit / term deposit]`: A bank fixed-term deposit
  account, which is popular for secure savings.
- `利息[lei6sik1|interest (on loan/deposit)]`: The interest earned on bank
  deposits or paid on loans.
- `息[sik1|interest / dividend]`: A short form for interest or dividend
  payments.
- `基金[gei1gam1|fund / mutual fund]`: Mutual funds or investment funds.
- `借[ze3|to borrow / lend]`: To borrow money from a bank/friend, or to lend to
  someone.
- `還[waan4|to return / repay]`: To return borrowed money or repay a loan.
- `跑數[paau2sou3|to hit a sales target / chase sales numbers]`: To chase sales
  targets or hit a financial quota (literally "running numbers").
- `長線[coeng4sin3|long term]`: A long-term time horizon for investments.
- `短線[dyun2sin3|short term]`: A short-term time horizon for trading.
- `身家[san1gaa1|net worth / personal fortune]`: A person's entire net worth,
  net fortune, or personal wealth.
- `最近[zeoi3gan6|recently / lately]`: Recently, lately.
- `噉樣[gam2joeng6|in this way / like this / this pattern]`: In this way, like
  this.

## 3. Situational Dialogues

### Dialogue 1: A Stock Market Rollercoaster

Two friends (`A` and `B`) discuss their recent stock market gains and losses
over coffee.

```dialog
A: 你[nei5|you] 最近[zeoi3gan6|recently / lately] 炒股[caau2gu2|to speculate in stocks / trade stocks] 賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money] 咗[zo2|completed action] 錢[cin2|money] 呀[aa3|particle]？
   === Have you made money speculating in stocks recently?
B: 唔[m4|not] 講[gong2|to speak / say] 啦[laa1|softening particle]！我[ngo5|I] 買入[maai5jap6|to buy in / purchase shares] 咗[zo2|completed action] 港股[gong2gu2|Hong Kong stocks / HK shares]，佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 跌[dit3|to fall / drop / decline] 得[dak1|particle expressing permission, possibility, capability, or adverbial degree] 咁[gam3|so] 慘[caam2|miserable / pitiful / tragic] 囉[lo1|obviousness particle]，我[ngo5|I] 全部[cyun4bou6|all / entirely] 蟹[haai5|stuck / trapped in a losing stock (slang)] 咗[zo2|completed action] 呀[aa3|particle]！
   === Don't talk about it! I bought Hong Kong stocks, and they fell so miserably that all of my shares are stuck!
A: 點會[dim2wui5|how could] 噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 呀[aa3|particle]？最近[zeoi3gan6|recently / lately] 唔[m4|not] 係[hai6|to be] 牛市[ngau4si5|bull market] 咩[me1|rhetorical doubt particle]？
   === How could it be like that? Isn't it a bull market recently?
B: 牛市[ngau4si5|bull market] 係[hai6|to be] 美股[mei5gu2|US stocks / US shares] 呀[aa3|particle]！美股[mei5gu2|US stocks / US shares] 爆升[baau3sing1|to skyrocket / experience explosive growth]，但係[daan6hai6|but / however] 港股[gong2gu2|Hong Kong stocks / HK shares] 俾[bei2|by / to give] 大鱷[daai6ngok6|market shark / big player] 賣出[maai6ceot1|to sell out / sell shares] 囉[lo1|obviousness particle]！
   === The bull market is only for US stocks! US stocks skyrocketed, but Hong Kong stocks were heavily sold out by market sharks!
A: 噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 你[nei5|you] 要[jiu3|need / want / must] 唔[m4|not] 要[jiu3|need / want / must] 賣出[maai6ceot1|to sell out / sell shares] 呀[aa3|particle]？
   === Then do you want to sell them out or not?
B: 唔[m4|not] 賣[maai6|to sell] 啦[laa1|softening particle]！我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|want to] 做[zou6|to do / make / perform] 長線[coeng4sin3|long term] 投資[tau4zi1|to invest / investment] 囉[lo1|obviousness particle]。
   === I won't sell! I want to do long-term investment.
A: 好[hou2|good / very / quite] 啦[laa1|softening particle]！希望[hei1mong6|to hope / wish / hope] 你[nei5|you] 唔[m4|not] 會[wui5|will] 蝕[sit6|to lose money / suffer a loss / deficit] 多[do1|many / much / more] 啲[di1|a bit / some / comparative suffix] 啦[laa1|softening particle]！
   === Alright! I hope you won't lose even more!
```

### Dialogue 2: Colleagues Discussing Personal Finance

Two colleagues (`A` and `B`) discuss saving money and managing bank accounts
during their lunch break.

```dialog
A: 你[nei5|you] 點樣[dim2joeng6|how / in what way] 慳[haan1|to save (money/time) / cut down / economize] 錢[cin2|money] 呀[aa3|particle]？我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|want to] 慳[haan1|to save (money/time) / cut down / economize] 錢[cin2|money] 做[zou6|to do / make / perform] 投資[tau4zi1|to invest / investment] 呀[aa3|particle]。
   === How do you save money? I want to save money to make investments.
B: 我[ngo5|I] 會[wui5|will / would / can] 放[fong3|to put / place] 錢[cin2|money] 喺[hai2|at / in / on (locative preposition)] 戶口[wu6hau2|account (bank/online)] 做[zou6|to do / make / perform] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit / term deposit] 囉[lo1|obviousness particle]，噉樣[gam2joeng6|in this way / like this / this pattern] 可以[ho2ji5|can] 賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money] 利息[lei6sik1|interest (on loan/deposit)] 嘛[maa3|asserting a known truth]。
   === I put money in my bank account for a fixed deposit. In this way, I can earn interest.
A: 係[hai6|to be] 咩[me1|rhetorical doubt particle]？最近[zeoi3gan6|recently / lately] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit / term deposit] 嘅[ge3|possessive / associative / focus particle] 息[sik1|interest / dividend] 都[dou1|also / both / all] 幾[gei2|quite / pretty / fairly] 好[hou2|good / very / quite] 呀[aa3|particle]！
   === Really? Recently, interest on fixed deposits is indeed quite good!
B: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes / indeed]！噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 你[nei5|you] 有冇[jau5mou5|is there / do you have (A-not-A question form)] 買[maai5|to buy] 基金[gei1gam1|fund / mutual fund] 呀[aa3|particle]？
   === Indeed! So do you buy mutual funds or not?
A: 我[ngo5|I] 買[maai5|to buy] 過[gwo3|experiential action suffix] 基金[gei1gam1|fund / mutual fund]，不過[bat1gwo3|but / however] 佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 跌[dit3|to fall / drop / decline] 咗[zo2|completed action suffix] 囉[lo1|obviousness particle]，我[ngo5|I] 就[zau6|then / right away / immediately] 賣出[maai6ceot1|to sell out / sell shares] 咗[zo2|completed action suffix] 囉[lo1|obviousness particle]。
   === I have bought mutual funds before, but it fell, so I just sold it out.
B: 噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 你[nei5|you] 都[dou1|also / both / all] 係[hai6|to be] 做[zou6|to do / make / perform] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit / term deposit] 慳[haan1|to save (money/time) / cut down / economize] 錢[cin2|money] 啦[laa1|softening particle]！
   === Then you should also do fixed deposits to save money!
```

## 4. Exercises

Test your understanding of Cantonese finance, investing, and money slang.

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following Cantonese slang terms describes being stuck or trapped in a losing stock position because the price fell?
  A) 牛市[ngau4si5|bull market]
  B) 大鱷[daai6ngok6|market shark / big player]
  C) 蟹[haai5|stuck / trapped in a losing stock (slang)]
  D) 爆升[baau3sing1|to skyrocket / experience explosive growth]
answer: C
explanation: 蟹[haai5|stuck / trapped in a losing stock (slang)] literally means "crab", but is highly popular slang in Hong Kong describing investors who are stuck holding losing shares.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb for "to earn / profit":
  我[ngo5|I] 炒股[caau2gu2|to speculate in stocks / trade stocks] ______ 咗[zo2|completed action] 好[hou2|very] 多[do1|many] 錢[cin2|money]。
answer: 賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money]
explanation: 賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money] is the colloquial Cantonese verb meaning to earn money or make a profit, while 蝕[sit6|to lose money / suffer a loss / deficit] means to lose money.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Translate the following sentence into colloquial Cantonese:
  "I put money in the bank account to do a fixed deposit to earn interest."
answer: 我[ngo5|I] 放[fong3|to put / place] 錢[cin2|money] 喺[hai2|at / in / on (locative preposition)] 戶口[wu6hau2|account (bank/online)] 做[zou6|to do / make / perform] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit / term deposit] 賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money] 利息[lei6sik1|interest (on loan/deposit)]。
explanation: 放[fong3|to put / place] means to put, 喺[hai2|at / in / on (locative preposition)] is the locative particle, 戶口[wu6hau2|account (bank/online)] is the bank account, 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit / term deposit] refers to fixed deposits, and 賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money] 利息[lei6sik1|interest (on loan/deposit)] means to earn interest.
```
