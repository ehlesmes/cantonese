In this chapter, we will master the correct classifiers (measure words) to use
when shopping for clothes, accessories, and shoes in Cantonese.

---

## 1. Essential Clothing & Shopping Classifiers

Using the correct classifier (measure word) is essential to sounding natural
when referring to different items in a shop or market. Avoid using
`個[go3|general classifier]` for everything! Here are the core classifiers for
shopping:

- `件[gin6|measure word for clothes, matters, and items]`: Used for upper-body
  clothing (shirts, jackets, coats).
  - Example: `呢[ni1|this]`
    `件[gin6|measure word for clothes, matters, and items]` `衫[saam1|clothes]`
    (this shirt)
- `條[tiu4|classifier for long, thin objects]`: Used for long, thin items,
  including pants, skirts, ties, and necklaces.
  - Example: `呢[ni1|this]` `條[tiu4|classifier for long, thin objects]`
    `褲[fu3|pants]` (these pants)
- `頂[ding2|measure word for hats]`: Exclusively used for hats and caps.
  - Example: `呢[ni1|this]` `頂[ding2|measure word for hats]` `帽[mou2|hat]`
    (this hat)
- `對[deoi3|pair (classifier)]`: Used for paired items like shoes, socks, or
  earrings.
  - Example: `呢[ni1|this]` `對[deoi3|pair (classifier)]` `鞋[haai4|shoes]`
    (these shoes)
- `隻[zek3|classifier for animals]`: Used to refer to just one item of a pair.
  - Example: `呢[ni1|this]` `隻[zek3|classifier for animals]` `鞋[haai4|shoes]`
    (this single shoe)

## 2. Situational Dialogue: Trying on Clothes

A customer (A) is browsing clothing items in a shop and talks to the assistant
(B).

```dialog
A: 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]，我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 睇[tai2|to look / watch] 呢[ni1|this] 件[gin6|measure word for clothes, matters, and items] 衫[saam1|clothes] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 呢[ni1|this] 條[tiu4|classifier for long, thin objects] 褲[fu3|pants] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Excuse me, I want to look at this shirt and these pants.
B: 好呀[hou2aa3|sure]！呢[ni1|this] 對[deoi3|pair (classifier)] 鞋[haai4|shoes] 都[dou1|also / all] 好[hou2|good / very] 靚[leng3|beautiful] 喎[wo3|sentence-final particle for surprise or warning]，你[nei5|you] 想唔想[soeng2 m4 soeng2|want or not (A-not-A question form)] 試[si3|to try] 埋[maai4|also] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Sure! This pair of shoes is also very nice, do you want to try them on too?
A: 呢[ni1|this] 對[deoi3|pair (classifier)] 鞋[haai4|shoes] 太[taai3|too] 大[daai6|big] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。有冇[jau5mou5|do you have?] 細[sai3|small] 啲[di1|a bit / some] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle]？
   === This pair of shoes is too big. Are there smaller ones?
B: 有[jau5|to have / there is]，等[dang2|wait] 我[ngo5|I] 攞[lo2|to fetch] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 你[nei5|you] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === Yes, let me get them for you.
```

## 3. Exercises

Use these exercises to test your understanding of Cantonese classifiers.

```exercise
question: |
  Which classifier should you use to ask for the price of a pair of shoes?
  A) 件[gin6|measure word for clothes, matters, and items]
  B) 條[tiu4|classifier for long, thin objects]
  C) 對[deoi3|pair (classifier)]
  D) 頂[ding2|measure word for hats]
answer: C
explanation: 對[deoi3|pair (classifier)] is the standard classifier for paired items like shoes or socks.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Which classifier is used for upper-body clothing like shirts and jackets?
  A) 條[tiu4|classifier for long, thin objects]
  B) 頂[ding2|measure word for hats]
  C) 件[gin6|measure word for clothes, matters, and items]
answer: C
explanation: 件[gin6|measure word for clothes, matters, and items] is the correct classifier for shirts, jackets, and coats.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Translate the following sentence into colloquial Cantonese:
  "Excuse me, how much is these pants?"
answer: 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]，呢[ni1|this] 條[tiu4|classifier for long, thin objects] 褲[fu3|pants] 幾錢[gei2 cin2|how much money]呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
explanation: 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please] means "excuse me", 呢[ni1|this] is "this", 條[tiu4|classifier for long, thin objects] is the classifier for trousers (褲[fu3|pants]), and 幾錢[gei2 cin2|how much money] is the spoken form of "how much".
```
