In this chapter, we will learn about **Stock Speculation & Investment Slang**.

Hong Kong has one of the world's most active financial markets, and trading
stocks is a popular national pastime. In daily conversation, talking about the
stock market uses highly expressive local slang.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Stock Market & Trading Slang

Here are the key slang terms and words for stock trading in Cantonese:

- `炒股[caau2gu2|to trade stocks]`: Speculating or actively trading in the stock
  market (literally "frying stocks").
- `股票[gu2piu3|stock]`: Stocks or shares.
- `港股[gong2gu2|Hong Kong stocks]`: Stocks traded on the Hong Kong Stock
  Exchange.
- `美股[mei5gu2|US stocks]`: Stocks traded on US exchanges.
- `散戶[saan2wu6|retail investor]`: Individual retail investors (literally
  "scattered households").
- `大鱷[daai6ngok6|market shark]`: Large institutional investors or hedge funds
  (literally "big crocodile").
- `開市[hoi1si5|market opening]`: Market opens.
- `收市[sau1si5|market closes]`: Market closes.
- `升[sing1|to rise]`: Prices or indices go up.
- `跌[dit3|to drop]`: Prices or indices go down.
- `爆升[baau3sing1|to skyrocket]`: Skyrocketing stock prices (literally
  "explosive rising").
- `牛市[ngau4si5|bull market]`: Bull market.
- `熊市[hung4si5|bear market]`: Bear market.
- `買入[maai5jap6|to buy in]`: Buying shares.
- `賣出[maai6ceot1|to sell out]`: Selling shares.
- `蟹[haai5|crab / trapped in a losing stock]`: Being trapped in a losing stock
  position because the price fell (literally "crab"). This is a verb and often
  paired with the completed aspect marker `咗[zo2|completed action marker]`.
  - _Example_: `我[ngo5|I]` `買[maai5|buy]` `嗰[go2|that]`
    `隻[zek3|classifier for animals]` `股票[gu2piu3|stock]`
    `蟹[haai5|crab / trapped in a losing stock]`
    `咗[zo2|completed action marker]`
    `呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]`:
    That stock I bought is stuck at a loss.
- `賺[zaan6|to earn]`: To make a profit or earn money.
- `蝕[sit6|to lose money]`: To lose money or suffer a financial loss.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: A Stock Market Rollercoaster

Two friends (A and B) discuss their recent stock market gains and losses over
coffee.

```dialog
A: 你[nei5|you] 最近[zeoi3gan6|recently] 炒股[caau2gu2|to trade stocks] 賺[zaan6|to earn] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 錢[cin4|money] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Have you made money speculating in stocks recently?
B: 唔[m4|not] 講[gong2|to speak] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！我[ngo5|I] 買入[maai5jap6|to buy in] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 港股[gong2gu2|Hong Kong stocks]，佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 跌[dit3|to drop] 得[dak1|okay / can / ready] 咁[gam3|so / to this degree] 慘[caam2|miserable] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]，我[ngo5|I] 全部[cyun4bou6|all] 蟹[haai5|crab / trapped in a losing stock] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
   === Don't talk about it! I bought Hong Kong stocks, and they fell so miserably that all of my shares are stuck!
A: 點會[dim2wui5|how could] 噉[gam2|like this / then] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？最近[zeoi3gan6|recently] 唔[m4|not] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 牛市[ngau4si5|bull market] 咩[me1|sentence-final particle for doubt / what]？
   === How could it be like that? Isn't it a bull market recently?
B: 牛市[ngau4si5|bull market] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 美股[mei5gu2|US stocks] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！美股[mei5gu2|US stocks] 爆升[baau3sing1|to skyrocket]，但係[daan6hai6|but] 港股[gong2gu2|Hong Kong stocks] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 大鱷[daai6ngok6|market shark] 賣出[maai6ceot1|to sell out] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]。
   === The bull market is only for US stocks! US stocks skyrocketed, but Hong Kong stocks were heavily sold out by market sharks!
A: 噉[gam2|like this / then] 你[nei5|you] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 唔[m4|not] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 賣出[maai6ceot1|to sell out] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Then do you want to sell them out or not?
B: 唔[m4|not] 賣[maai6|to sell] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 做[zou6|to do] 長線[coeng4sin3|long term] 投資[tau4zi1|to invest] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]。
   === I won't sell! I want to do long-term investment, obviously.
A: 好[hou2|good / very] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！希望[hei1mong6|to hope] 你[nei5|you] 唔[m4|not] 會[wui5|will / can] 蝕[sit6|to lose money] 多[do1|many] 啲[di1|a bit / some] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！
   === Alright! I hope you won't lose even more!
```

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of stock market slangs and investment terms in
Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following Cantonese terms describes being stuck or trapped in a losing stock position?
  A) 牛市[ngau4si5|bull market]
  B) 大鱷[daai6ngok6|market shark]
  C) 蟹[haai5|crab / trapped in a losing stock]
  D) 爆升[baau3sing1|to skyrocket]
answer: C
explanation: |
  `蟹[haai5|crab / trapped in a losing stock]` (literally "crab") is highly popular slang in Hong Kong describing investors stuck holding losing shares.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following means "large institutional players or hedge funds"?
  A) 散戶[saan2wu6|retail investor]
  B) 大鱷[daai6ngok6|market shark]
  C) 股票[gu2piu3|stock]
  D) 熊市[hung4si5|bear market]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `大鱷[daai6ngok6|market shark]` (literally "big crocodile") refers to large institutional players or hedge funds who manipulate market prices.
```
