In this chapter, we will learn about **Smartphones, Apps & Digital Payments**.

Hong Kong social media and app culture has created colorful spoken vocabulary.
Many terms are direct Cantonese phonetics of English loanwords, and digital
payment apps are often used directly as verbs.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Apps & Social Media Slang

Hong Kongers use a variety of specialized terms for apps and messaging:

- `App[ep1|app]`: Pronounced like the English "app" but with a classic Cantonese
  short, clipped tone `ep1`.
- `些牙[se1aa4|to share (loanword)]`: A popular loanword from English "share,"
  meaning to share a link, photo, or post.
- `IG[ai1zi1|Instagram]`: Instagram.
- `FB[ef1bi1|Facebook]`: Facebook.
- `WhatsApp[wat1sep1|WhatsApp]`: WhatsApp, also used as a verb.
  - _Example_: `你[nei5|you]` `陣間[zan6gaan1|later]`
    `WhatsApp[wat1sep1|WhatsApp]` `我[ngo5|I]`
    `啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]`:
    WhatsApp me later.
- `send[sen1|to send (loanword)]`: To send or transmit.
- `send[sen1|to send (loanword)]`: To send.
- `覆[fuk1|to reply]`: To reply to a text or message.
- `留言[lau4jin4|to leave a message]`: To comment or leave a message.
- `短訊[dyun2seon3|text message]`: A text message.
- `錄音[luk6jam1|voice recording]`: A voice note, or recording audio.
- `片[pin2|video / movie]`: A video or short clip.
- `Cap圖[kep1tou4|to take a screenshot]`: To take a screenshot.
- `已讀不回[ji5duk6bat1wui4|to leave on read]`: Leaving someone on read.

## 2. Cashless & Digital Payments

Hong Kong has rapidly adopted cashless payments, bringing new colloquial verbs:

- `PayMe[pei1mi1|PayMe]`: A ubiquitous local payment app, often used as a verb.
  - _Example_: `我[ngo5|I]` `轉頭[zyun2tau4|later]` `PayMe[pei1mi1|PayMe]`
    `俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let]` `你[nei5|you]`: I will PayMe you
    later.
- `轉數快[zyun3sou3faai3|FPS (Faster Payment System)]`: Faster Payment System
  for instant bank transfers.
- `支付寶[zi1fu6bou2|Alipay]`: Alipay digital wallet.

## 3. Situational Dialogue: Texting & Paying Back

Two friends, Sau-han (A) and Wing-kit (B), coordinate their plans and settle a
bill.

```dialog
A: Wing-kit，你[nei5|you] 睇[tai2|to look / watch] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 我[ngo5|I] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] IG[ai1zi1|Instagram] 些牙[se1aa4|to share (loanword)] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 你[nei5|you] 嗰[go2|that] 條[tiu4|classifier for long, thin objects] 片[pin2|video / movie] 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Wing-kit, have you watched that video I shared with you on Instagram yet?
B: 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！ 我[ngo5|I] 啱啱[aam1aam1|just now] 覆[fuk1|to reply] 緊[gan2|continuous aspect marker] 訊息[seon3sik1|message] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。 你[nei5|you] send[sen1|to send (loanword)] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 我[ngo5|I] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Not yet! I was just replying to messages. Did you send it to me?
A: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes]，我[ngo5|I] WhatsApp[wat1sep1|WhatsApp] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 你[nei5|you] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！ 你[nei5|you] 記得[gei3dak1|to remember] 覆[fuk1|to reply] 我[ngo5|I] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Yes, I WhatsApped you already! Remember to reply to me.
B: 冇問題[mou5man6tai4|no problem]！ 我[ngo5|I] 轉頭[zyun2tau4|later] 睇[tai2|to look / watch] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === No problem! I'll watch it in a moment.
A: 同埋[tung4maai4|and]，你[nei5|you] 尋日[cam4jat6|yesterday] 去街[heoi3gaai1|to go out]，仲[zung6|still / yet] 未[mei6|not yet] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 錢[cin2|money] 我[ngo5|I] 喎[wo3|sentence-final particle for surprise or warning]！
   === Also, you went out yesterday and still haven't given me the money!
B: 哎呀[aai1jaa3|interjection of surprise or annoyance]！ 唔好意思[m4hou2ji3si1|excuse me / sorry]。 我[ngo5|I] 依家[ji1gaa1|now] PayMe[pei1mi1|PayMe] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 你[nei5|you] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]，好唔好[hou2 m4 hou2|is that good] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Oh! Sorry. I will PayMe you now, is that good?
A: 好[hou2|good / very] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！ 或者[waak6ze2|perhaps / or] 用[jung6|to use] 轉數快[zyun3sou3faai3|FPS (Faster Payment System)] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 我[ngo5|I] 都[dou1|also / all] 可以[ho2ji5|can] 㗎[gaa3|sentence-final particle indicating assertion]。
   === Sure! Or transferring to me using FPS is also fine.
```

## 4. Exercises

Test your understanding of app terminology and cashless payments in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following refers to a local instant payment app commonly used as a verb in spoken Cantonese?
  A) WhatsApp[wat1sep1|WhatsApp]
  B) PayMe[pei1mi1|PayMe]
  C) IG[ai1zi1|Instagram]
  D) FB[ef1bi1|Facebook]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `PayMe[pei1mi1|PayMe]` is the name of a local Hong Kong payment app, which is commonly verbalized as "to PayMe someone".
```

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb for "to reply to a message":
  你[nei5|you] 記得[gei3dak1|to remember] ______ 我[ngo5|I] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
answer: 覆[fuk1|to reply]
explanation: |
  `覆[fuk1|to reply]` is the standard Cantonese verb used to mean replying to messages or emails.
```
