In this chapter, we will learn about **Gathering Plans & Social Slang**.

Making plans and managing social gatherings comes with its own local slang in
Hong Kong.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Gathering Plans & Shorthand

Here is the essential vocabulary for organizing and describing social events:

- `局[guk6|gathering / social event / plan]`: A gathering, social plan, or
  get-together (e.g., a drinking or dinner plan).
- `直落[zik6lok6|to continue onto another venue / next round without stopping]`:
  To continue to another venue or the next round of a night out without
  stopping.
- `下場[haa6coeng4|next venue / afterparty / next round]`: The next venue,
  round, or afterparty of the night (literally "next scene").
- `放飛機[fong3fei1gei1|to stand someone up / break a promise]`: To stand
  someone up, cancel plans last minute, or fail to show up.
- `飛機王[fei1gei1wong4|chronic flake / person who always stands others up]`: A
  chronic flake; someone who habitually stands people up.
- `派對[paai3deoi3|party]`: A party or festive social gathering.
- `吹水[ceoi1seoi2|to chat / shoot the breeze / gossip]`: To chat, shoot the
  breeze, or gossip with friends.
- `潛水[cim4seoi2|to go offline / go silent]`: To go offline, disappear from
  chat groups, or go silent (literally "to dive/snorkel").
- `得閒[dak1haan4|free (spare time)]`: Free, having spare time.
- `AA制[ei1ei1zai3|going Dutch / splitting the bill]`: Going Dutch or splitting
  the bill evenly among the group.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: Planning Saturday Night

Two friends, Sau-han (A) and Wing-kit (B), discuss their plans for Saturday.

```dialog
A: 星期六[sing1kei4luk6|Saturday] 你[nei5|you] 得閒[dak1haan4|free (spare time)] 唔[m4|not / no (negative prefix)] 得閒[dak1haan4|free (spare time)] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？我哋[ngo5dei6|we / us] 有[jau5|to have] 個[go3|general measure word / individual / unit] 唱[coeng3|to sing]K 嘅[ge3|possessive particle / sentence-final particle asserting a fact] 局[guk6|gathering / social event / plan] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Are you free this Saturday? We have a karaoke gathering plan.
B: 唱[coeng3|to sing]K 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？好呀[hou2aa3|sure]！有[jau5|to have] 邊個[bin1go3|who / which] 去[heoi3|to go] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Karaoke? Sure! Who is going?
A: 我哋[ngo5dei6|we / us] 差唔多[caa1m4do1|almost / about / more or less] 十[sap6|ten] 個[go3|general measure word / individual / unit] 人[jan4|person / people] 去[heoi3|to go] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。不過[bat1gwo3|but / however] David 成日[seng4jat6|always] 放飛機[fong3fei1gei1|to stand someone up / break a promise]，真係[zan1hai6|really / truly] 飛機王[fei1gei1wong4|chronic flake / person who always stands others up] 嚟[lei4|sentence-final particle indicating that something is in a certain state] 嘅[ge3|possessive particle / sentence-final particle asserting a fact]！
   === Almost ten of us are going. But David always stands people up, he is really a chronic flake!
B: 哈哈[haa1haa1|haha]！噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 我哋[ngo5dei6|we / us] 唱[coeng3|to sing] 完[jyun4|finished / complete] 之後[zi1hau6|after / afterwards] 會[wui5|will / can / able to] 唔[m4|not / no (negative prefix)] 會[wui5|will / can / able to] 直落[zik6lok6|to continue onto another venue / next round without stopping] 去[heoi3|to go] 下場[haa6coeng4|next venue / afterparty / next round] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Haha! Then after we finish singing, will we keep going straight to the next venue?
A: 唱[coeng3|to sing] 完[jyun4|finished / complete] 都[dou1|also / even / already] 幾[gei2|quite / pretty / fairly] 晏[aan3|late]，或者[waak6ze2|perhaps / or] 去[heoi3|to go] 食[sik6|to eat] 宵夜[siu1je2|late night snack] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle showing obviousness, resignation, or 'of course']。
   === It will be quite late after singing, maybe we can go for a late night snack.
```

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of gathering terms and flaking slang in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following refers to a person who habitually stands people up or cancels plans last minute?
  A) 飛機王[fei1gei1wong4|chronic flake]
  B) 派對[paai3deoi3|party]
  C) 吹水[ceoi1seoi2|to chat]
answer: A
explanation: |
  `飛機王[fei1gei1wong4|chronic flake]` comes from the phrase `放飛機[fong3fei1gei1|to stand someone up]` and describes a chronic flake.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Which term is used when friends decide to go Dutch or split the bill evenly?
  A) 直落[zik6lok6|to continue onto another venue]
  B) AA制[ei1ei1zai3|going Dutch]
  C) 潛水[cim4seoi2|to go offline]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `AA制[ei1ei1zai3|going Dutch]` is the standard colloquial term for splitting a bill.
```
