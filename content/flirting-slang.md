colloquial terms for crushes and unrequited admiration in Hong Kong.

---

## 1. The Dynamics of Flirting & "Soldiering"

Cantonese pop culture and student dynamics have created distinct humorous terms
for crushes and unrequited admiration.

### Crushes & Ideals

- `暗戀[ngam3lyun2|to have a crush on]`: To have a secret crush on someone.
- `女神[neoi5san4|ideal woman]`: An ideal woman or female crush.
- `男神[naam4san4|ideal man]`: An ideal man or male crush.

### The Concept of "Soldiering" (`收兵[sau1bing1|to collect platonic admirers]`)

One of the most unique local slang metaphors is:

- `收兵[sau1bing1|to collect platonic admirers]`: The act of keeping platonic
  admirers around to do favors, buy gifts, or drive them around, without ever
  intending to date them.
- `兵[bing1|platonic admirer / soldier]`: A platonic admirer who is led on and
  does favors for their crush (the "goddess") in the hope of eventually dating
  them.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: The Goddess and Her Soldier

Two classmates discuss a friend's romantic situation.

```dialog
   === Hey, do you have a secret crush on her?
B: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes]，佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 我[ngo5|I] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle] 女神[neoi5san4|ideal woman] 嘛[maa3|sentence-final particle indicating a statement is an obvious truth or reminder]！
   === Yes, she is my goddess, obviously!

   === In that case, did you help her buy food?

   === Yes, I always do favors for her.
A: 哈哈[haa1haa1|haha]！ 你[nei5|you] 真係[zan1hai6|really] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle] 兵[bing1|platonic admirer / soldier] 喇[laa3|sentence-final particle for change of state]！佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 最[zeoi3|most] 鍾意[zung1ji3|to like] 收兵[sau1bing1|to collect platonic admirers] 㗎啦[gaa3laa1|sentence-final particle combination indicating strong assertion or finality]！
   === Haha! You are really her soldier! She loves collecting admirers most!
```
