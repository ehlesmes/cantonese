In this chapter, we will learn about **Apartment Rentals & Layouts**.

Hong Kong’s high-density urban environment has led to unique residential
structures. When navigating the housing market, it is essential to distinguish
between different types of buildings and understand the rental process.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Types of Housing in Hong Kong

When flat-hunting, you will encounter various housing options:

- `唐樓[tong4lau2|traditional low-rise walk-up building]`: These are older
  low-rise buildings (typically 5 to 8 stories) constructed without a
  `升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator / lift]`. They are famous for their high
  ceilings and historical value.
- `洋樓[joeng4lau2|modern high-rise building with elevator]`: These are modern
  mid-to-high-rise residential buildings equipped with a
  `升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator / lift]` and usually have a security guard at
  the lobby.
- `套房[tou3fong2|studio flat / ensuite bedroom]`: A studio apartment or a
  single self-contained bedroom containing its own private bathroom.
- `劏房[tong1fong2|subdivided flat / partitioned room]`: A flat that has been
  partitioned or split into multiple tiny individual units.
- `公屋[gung1uk1|public housing]`: Public housing estates.
- `屋[uk1|house / room]`: A general term for a house or room.
- `樓[lau2|building / flat / apartment / floor]`: A generic term that can mean a
  building, a flat, an apartment, or a floor.

## 2. Flat-Hunting & Rental Terminology

The process of finding and renting a flat involves specific vocabulary:

- `搵樓[wan2lau2|to look for apartments / flat-hunting]`: The action of
  searching for an apartment.
- `睇樓[tai2lau2|to view apartments / flats]`: Going to view apartments in
  person.
- `租[zou1|to rent / lease]`: To rent or lease a property.
- `租客[zou1haak3|tenant / renter]`: The tenant.
- `業主[jip6zyu2|landlord / property owner]`: The landlord or property owner.
- `租金[zou1gam1|rent payment / rental price]`: The monthly rent.
- `按金[on3gam1|deposit]`: Security deposit.
- `上期[soeng5kei4|rent paid in advance (first month rent)]`: First month of
  rent paid in advance.
- `兩按一上[loeng5on3jat1soeng5|two months deposit and one month rent in advance]`:
  The standard Hong Kong upfront payment scheme.
- `合約[hap6joek3|contract / agreement]`: The formal lease contract.
- `佣金[jung2gam1|commission fee]`: Agency commission fee.
- `屋苑[uk1jyun2|housing estate / complex]`: A housing estate or complex.
- `管理費[gun2lei5fai3|management fee]`: Building management fee.

## 4. Exercises

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following describes a traditional low-rise walk-up building that does not have an elevator?
  A) 洋樓[joeng4lau2|modern high-rise building with elevator]
  B) 唐樓[tong4lau2|traditional low-rise walk-up building]
  C) 劏房[tong1fong2|subdivided flat / partitioned room]
  D) 套房[tou3fong2|studio flat / ensuite bedroom]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `唐樓[tong4lau2|traditional low-rise walk-up building]` refers specifically to older, low-rise walk-up buildings in Hong Kong without elevator access.
```
