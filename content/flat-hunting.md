## 1. Flat-Hunting & Rental Terminology

The process of finding and renting a flat involves specific vocabulary:

- `搵樓[wan2lau2|to look for apartments]`: The action of searching for an
  apartment.
- `睇樓[tai2lau2|to view apartments]`: Going to view apartments in person.
- `租[zou1|to rent]`: To rent or lease a property.
- `租客[zou1haak3|tenant]`: The tenant.
- `業主[jip6zyu2|landlord]`: The landlord or property owner.
- `租金[zou1gam1|rent]`: The monthly rent.
- `按金[on3gam1|deposit]`: Security deposit.
- `上期[soeng6kei4|rent paid in advance (first month rent)]`: First month of
  rent paid in advance.
- `兩按一上[loeng5on3jat1soeng6|two months deposit and one month rent in advance]`:
  The standard Hong Kong upfront payment scheme.
- `合約[hap6joek3|contract]`: The formal lease contract.
- `佣金[jung2gam1|commission fee]`: Agency commission fee.
- `屋苑[uk1jyun2|housing estate]`: A housing estate or complex.
- `管理費[gun2lei5fai3|management fee]`: Building management fee.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: Finding an Apartment with an Agent

A client (A) meets with a real estate agent (B) to discuss requirements and view
apartments.

```dialog
A: 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]，我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 搵樓[wan2lau2|to look for apartments]。我[ngo5|I] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle] 要求[jiu1kau4|requirement] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 附近[fu6gan6|nearby] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 方便[fong1bin6|convenient]，最[zeoi3|most] 好[hou2|good / very] 有[jau5|to have / there is] 升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle] 洋樓[joeng4lau2|modern high-rise building with elevator]。
   === Excuse me, I want to look for apartments. My requirement is that the neighborhood must be convenient, preferably a modern high-rise building with an elevator.
B: 冇[mou5|to not have] 問題[man6tai4|problem / question]！呢度[ni1dou6|here] 有[jau5|to have / there is] 個[go3|general classifier] 單位[daan1wai2|apartment]。佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 有[jau5|to have / there is] 兩[loeng5|two] 間[gaan1|classifier for buildings] 房[fong2|room] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === No problem! There is a unit here. It has two rooms.
A: 好似[hou2ci5|seems like] 幾[gei2|quite] 好[hou2|good / very] 喎[wo3|sentence-final particle for surprise or warning]！租金[zou1gam1|rent] 幾多[gei2do1|how many / how much] 錢[cin2|money] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === That sounds pretty good! How much is the rent?
B: 租金[zou1gam1|rent] 一[jat1|one] 萬[maan6|ten thousand] 五[ng5|five] 千[cin1|thousand] 蚊[man1|dollar] 一[jat1|one] 個[go3|general classifier] 月[jyut6|month]，要[jiu3|to want / need] 兩按一上[loeng5on3jat1soeng6|two months deposit and one month rent in advance]，同埋[tung4maai4|and] 簽[cim1|to sign] 合約[hap6joek3|contract] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle] 時候[si4hau6|time] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 半[bun3|half] 個[go3|general classifier] 租金[zou1gam1|rent] 做[zou6|to do] 佣金[jung2gam1|commission fee] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === The rent is fifteen thousand dollars a month. It requires two months deposit and one month rent in advance, and when signing the contract, you pay half a month's rent as a commission fee.
A: 噉[gam2|like this / then] 我哋[ngo5dei6|we] 幾時[gei2si4|when] 可以[ho2ji5|can] 去[heoi3|go] 睇樓[tai2lau2|to view apartments] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 睇[tai2|to look / watch] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 先[sin1|first]。
   === Then when can we go to view the flat? I want to view it first.
B: 聽日[ting1jat6|tomorrow] 可以[ho2ji5|can] 去[heoi3|go] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！我[ngo5|I] 幫[bong1|to help] 你[nei5|you] 約[joek3|to make an appointment] 業主[jip6zyu2|landlord] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === We can go tomorrow! I will help you make an appointment with the landlord.
```

  我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] ______ 呢[ni1|this] 間[gaan1|classifier for buildings] 房[fong2|room].
answer: 租[zou1|to rent]

  `租[zou1|to rent]` is the colloquial verb for renting or leasing a property.
