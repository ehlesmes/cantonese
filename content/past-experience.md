## 1. Talking About Past Experience: Aspect Particle `過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than]`

To express that you **have done something before** at least once in your life
(the experiential aspect), Cantonese uses the particle
`過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than]`.

### The Experiential Structure

The particle `過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than]` is placed
**directly after the main verb**:

> **Formula:** [Subject] + [Verb] +
> `過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than]` + [Object]

Let's look at some examples:

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 讀[duk6|to study / read aloud] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 大學[daai6hok6|university] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
===
I have studied at university before.
```

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 走[zau2|to leave] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 堂[tong4|class] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]！
===
I have skipped class before, obviously!
```

### Negating Experiences: `冇[mou5|to not have]`

To say that you **have never done** something, place `冇[mou5|to not have]`
before the verb, but **keep** the
`過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than]` after the verb!

> **Formula:** [Subject] + `冇[mou5|to not have]` + [Verb] +
> `過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than]` + [Object]

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 冇[mou5|to not have] 飲[jam2|drink] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 凍[dung3|cold] 奶茶[naai5caa4|milk tea] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
===
I have never drunk iced milk tea before.
```

To ask an experiential question ("Have you ever...?"), use the A-not-A style
question or standard question particles:

```cantonese
你[nei5|you] 去[heoi3|go] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 邊度[bin1dou6|where] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
===
Where have you been before?
```

## 2. Vocabulary Spotlight: Education & Schooling

Here is some essential vocabulary related to schooling and exams:

- `大學[daai6hok6|university]` — university
- `讀書[duk6syu1|to study]` — studying / to study
- `考試[haau2si3|to take an exam]` — exams / to take an exam
- `走堂[zau2tong4|to skip class]` — skipping class
- `英文[jing1man4|English language]` — English language

## 3. Situational Dialogue: University Life

In this conversation, Wing-kit (A) and Sau-han (B) are talking in a café about
their university experiences and exams. Pay close attention to how they use
modal verbs, past experience markers, and potential suffixes.

> [!NOTE] In the dialogue below, you will see the verbal suffix
> `吓[haa5|brief action marker]`. When attached to a verb, it means doing the
> action "a bit" or "casually" (e.g. `休息[jau1sik1|to rest]`
> `吓[haa5|brief action marker]` means "rest a bit").

```dialog
A: 喂[wai2|hey! / hello]，Sau-han！你[nei5|you] 讀[duk6|to study / read aloud] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 大學[daai6hok6|university] 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Hey, Sau-han! Have you studied at university before?
B: 我[ngo5|I] 讀[duk6|to study / read aloud] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。我[ngo5|I] 好[hou2|good / very] 鍾意[zung1ji3|to like] 讀書[duk6syu1|to study] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]！
   === I have studied before. I really like studying, obviously!
A: 噉[gam2|like this / then] 你[nei5|you] 識唔識[sik1 m4 sik1|do you know how to?] 講[gong2|to speak] 英文[jing1man4|English language] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === In that case, do you know how to speak English?
B: 我[ngo5|I] 識[sik1|to know how to] 講[gong2|to speak] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]，我[ngo5|I] 只係[zi2hai6|only] 識[sik1|to know how to] 講[gong2|to speak] 廣東話[gwong2dung1waa6|Cantonese language] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 英文[jing1man4|English language] 啫[ze1|only / just]。
   === I know how to speak it; I only know how to speak Cantonese and English.
A: 噉[gam2|like this / then] 你[nei5|you] 走[zau2|to leave] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 堂[tong4|class] 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Then have you skipped class before?
B: 我[ngo5|I] 冇[mou5|to not have] 走[zau2|to leave] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 堂[tong4|class] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！ 你[nei5|you] 聽日[ting1jat6|tomorrow] 要唔要[jiu3 m4 jiu3|do you want?] 考試[haau2si3|to take an exam] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === I have never skipped class before! Do you need to take an exam tomorrow?
A: 要[jiu3|to want / need] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！我[ngo5|I] 今日[gam1jat6|today] 溫[wan1|to revise] 唔到[m4dou2|cannot] 書[syu1|book] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]。
   === Yes, I do! But I cannot manage to study today, obviously.
B: 點解[dim2gaai2|why] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Why?
A: 因為[jan1wai6|because] 我[ngo5|I] 唔舒服[m4syu1fuk6|unwell]，睇[tai2|to look / watch] 唔到[m4dou2|cannot] 字[zi6|word / five-minute interval] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Because I am feeling unwell; I cannot manage to read the words.
B: 噉[gam2|like this / then] 你[nei5|you] 可以[ho2ji5|can] 休息[jau1sik1|to rest] 吓[haa5|brief action marker]。聽日[ting1jat6|tomorrow] 先[sin1|first] 溫書[wan1syu1|to study] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！
   === In that case you can rest a bit. Study tomorrow instead!
A: 好[hou2|good / very] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please] 你[nei5|you] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === Sure! Thank you.
```
