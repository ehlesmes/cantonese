In this chapter, we will learn about **Seeing the Doctor & Treatment**.

When home remedies are not enough, consulting a doctor, taking the correct
medication, and resting are key parts of getting well.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Seeing the Doctor & Treatment

When home remedies are not enough, consulting a doctor and taking the correct
medication is the next step:

- `睇醫生[tai2ji1sang1|to see a doctor]`: Literally "to look at the doctor,"
  meaning to go see a doctor.
- `睇西醫[tai2 sai1 ji1|to see a Western doctor]`: To see a Western medicine
  doctor.
- `睇中醫[tai2 zung1 ji1|to see a Chinese doctor]`: To see a traditional Chinese
  medicine doctor.
- `醫生[ji1sang1|doctor / medical practitioner]`: A doctor.
- `藥[joek6|medicine / drug]`: Medicine.
- `食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine]`: Literally "to eat medicine," meaning to
  take medication.
- `開[hoi1|to write (a prescription) / prescribe / open / start]`: In a medical
  context, to prescribe.
- `好返[hou2faan1|to recover / get well]`: To get well or recover.
- `頂住[ding2zyu6|to hold on / hang in there / endure]`: To hold on or hang in
  there.
- `抖[dau2|to rest / take a break]`: To rest or take a break.
- `抖吓[dau2haa5|to rest a bit / take a brief break]`: To rest a bit.
- `檢查[gim2caa4|to examine / check / inspect]`: To examine or check.
- `聽落[teng1lok6|sounds like]`: Sounds like, hearing it.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: At the Clinic

Ah Ming consults a doctor about his stomach ailments.

```dialog
A: 阿明[aa3 ming4|Ah Ming]，你[nei5|you] 邊度[bin1dou6|where] 唔舒服[m4syu1fuk6|uncomfortable / unwell / sick] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Ah Ming, where do you feel unwell?
B: 醫生[ji1sang1|doctor / medical practitioner]，我[ngo5|I / me] 琴晚[kam4maan5|last night] 開始[hoi1ci2|to start / begin] 頭暈[tau4wan4|dizzy / lightheaded] 同埋[tung4maai4|and / with / together with] 肚痛[tou5tung3|stomach ache / abdominal pain]，今日[gam1jat6|today] 朝早[ziu1zou2|morning / in the morning] 仲[zung6|still / yet] 痾[o1|to have diarrhea / excrete] 同埋[tung4maai4|and / with / together with] 嘔[au2|to vomit / throw up] 咗[zo2|verbal aspect suffix indicating completed action (perfective aspect)] 幾[gei2|quite / pretty / fairly] 次[ci3|time / occurrence (classifier)] 添[tim1|as well / also / particle]。
   === Doctor, I started feeling dizzy and having a stomach ache last night. This morning I even had diarrhea and threw up several times.
A: 好[hou2|good / fine / very / quite]，等[dang2|to wait] 我[ngo5|I / me] 幫[bong1|to help / for / on behalf of] 你[nei5|you] 檢查[gim2caa4|to examine / check / inspect] 吓[haa5|verbal suffix indicating a brief action or attempt] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。你[nei5|you] 應該[jing1goi1|should / ought to] 係[hai6|to be (am/is/are) / yes] 食[sik6|to eat / to take] 錯[co3|wrong / incorrect / mistake] 嘢[je5|thing / stuff / matter] 中[zung3|to hit / catch (an illness)] 咗[zo2|verbal aspect suffix indicating completed action (perfective aspect)] 腸胃炎[coeng4wai6jim4|gastroenteritis / stomach flu] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Okay, let me examine you. You probably ate something bad and caught gastroenteritis.
B: 咁[gam3|so / such / that] 嚴重[jim4zung6|serious / severe / grave] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？醫生[ji1sang1|doctor / medical practitioner]，我[ngo5|I / me] 使唔使[sai2m4sai2|need or not / do you need (A-not-A question form)] 食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Is it that serious? Doctor, do I need to take medicine?
A: 要[jiu3|to want / need / require] 食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。我[ngo5|I / me] 會[wui5|will / can / able to] 開[hoi1|to write (a prescription) / prescribe / open / start] 啲[di1|a bit / some / comparative suffix] 藥[joek6|medicine / drug] 俾[bei2|to give / for / let / allow] 你[nei5|you]，你[nei5|you] 記得[gei3dak1|to remember] 一[jat1|one] 日[jat6|day / date] 食[sik6|to eat / to take] 三[saam1|three] 次[ci3|time / occurrence (classifier)]。
   === Yes, you need to take medicine. I will prescribe some medicine for you, and remember to take it three times a day.
```

  你[nei5|you] 記得[gei3dak1|remember] 每日[mui5jat6|every day] ______ 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。

  `食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine]` literally translates to "eat medicine" and is the standard Cantonese phrase for taking medication.
