In this chapter, we will learn about **Seeing the Doctor & Treatment**.

When home remedies are not enough, consulting a doctor, taking the correct
medication, and resting are key parts of getting well.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Seeing the Doctor & Treatment

When home remedies are not enough, consulting a doctor and taking the correct
medication is the next step:

- `睇醫生[tai2ji1sang1|to see a doctor]`: Literally "to look at the doctor,"
  meaning to go see a doctor.
- `睇西醫[tai2 sai1 ji1|to see a Western doctor]`: To see a Western medicine
  doctor.
- `睇中醫[tai2 zung1 ji1|to see a Chinese doctor]`: To see a traditional Chinese
  medicine doctor.
- `醫生[ji1sang1|doctor]`: A doctor.
- `藥[joek6|medicine]`: Medicine.
- `食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine]`: Literally "to eat medicine," meaning to
  take medication.
- `開[hoi1|to open / turn on / prescribe]`: In a medical context, to prescribe.
- `好返[hou2faan1|to recover]`: To get well or recover.
- `頂住[ding2zyu6|to endure]`: To hold on or hang in there.
- `抖[dau2|to rest]`: To rest or take a break.
- `抖吓[dau2haa5|to rest a bit]`: To rest a bit.
- `檢查[gim2caa4|to examine]`: To examine or check.
- `聽落[teng1lok6|sounds like]`: Sounds like, hearing it.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: At the Clinic

Ah Ming consults a doctor about his stomach ailments.

```dialog
A: 阿明[aa3 ming4|Ah Ming]，你[nei5|you] 邊度[bin1dou6|where] 唔舒服[m4syu1fuk6|unwell] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Ah Ming, where do you feel unwell?
B: 醫生[ji1sang1|doctor]，我[ngo5|I] 琴晚[kam4maan5|last night] 開始[hoi1ci2|to start] 頭暈[tau4wan4|dizzy] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 肚痛[tou5tung3|stomach ache]，今日[gam1jat6|today] 朝早[ziu1zou2|in the morning] 仲[zung6|still / yet] 痾[o1|to excrete] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 嘔[au2|to vomit] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 幾[gei2|quite] 次[ci3|time (occurrence)] 添[tim1|particle indicating addition]。
   === Doctor, I started feeling dizzy and having a stomach ache last night. This morning I even had diarrhea and threw up several times.
A: 好[hou2|good / very]，等[dang2|wait] 我[ngo5|I] 幫[bong1|to help] 你[nei5|you] 檢查[gim2caa4|to examine] 吓[haa5|brief action marker] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。你[nei5|you] 應該[jing1goi1|should] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 食[sik6|to eat] 錯[co3|wrong] 嘢[je5|thing] 中[zung3|to win / catch an illness] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 腸胃炎[coeng4wai6jim4|stomach flu] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Okay, let me examine you. You probably ate something bad and caught gastroenteritis.
B: 咁[gam3|so / to this degree] 嚴重[jim4zung6|severe] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？醫生[ji1sang1|doctor]，我[ngo5|I] 使唔使[sai2m4sai2|do you need?] 食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Is it that serious? Doctor, do I need to take medicine?
A: 要[jiu3|to want / need] 食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。我[ngo5|I] 會[wui5|will / can] 開[hoi1|to open / turn on / prescribe] 啲[di1|a bit / some] 藥[joek6|medicine] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 你[nei5|you]，你[nei5|you] 記得[gei3dak1|to remember] 一[jat1|one] 日[jat6|day] 食[sik6|to eat] 三[saam1|three] 次[ci3|time (occurrence)]。
   === Yes, you need to take medicine. I will prescribe some medicine for you, and remember to take it three times a day.
```

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of doctor visits and treatments in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb phrase for "to take medicine":
  你[nei5|you] 記得[gei3dak1|to remember] 每日[mui5jat6|every day] ______ 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
answer: 食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine]
explanation: |
  `食藥[sik6joek6|to take medicine]` literally translates to "eat medicine" and is the standard Cantonese phrase for taking medication.
```
