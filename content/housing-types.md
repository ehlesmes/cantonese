Hong Kong’s high-density urban environment has led to unique residential
structures. When navigating the housing market, it is essential to distinguish
between different types of buildings.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Types of Housing in Hong Kong

When looking for a place to live, you will encounter various housing options:

- `唐樓[tong4lau2|traditional low-rise walk-up building]`: These are older
  low-rise buildings (typically 5 to 8 stories) constructed without a
  `升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator]`. They are famous for their high ceilings and
  historical value.
- `洋樓[joeng4lau2|modern high-rise building with elevator]`: These are modern
  mid-to-high-rise residential buildings equipped with a
  `升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator]` and usually have a security guard at the
  lobby.
- `套房[tou3fong2|studio flat]`: A studio apartment or a single self-contained
  bedroom containing its own private bathroom.
- `劏房[tong1fong2|subdivided flat]`: A flat that has been partitioned or split
  into multiple tiny individual units.
- `公屋[gung1uk1|public housing]`: Public housing estates.
- `屋[uk1|house]`: A general term for a house or room.
- `樓[lau2|building / flat]`: A generic term that can mean a building, a flat,
  an apartment, or a floor.
