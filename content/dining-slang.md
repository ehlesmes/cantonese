## 1. Local Café Slang: Ordering a `靚仔[leng3zai2|handsome boy / plain white rice]`

If you listen closely to a busy café waiter shouting orders to the kitchen, you
might hear them call out `靚仔[leng3zai2|handsome boy / plain white rice]`.

In daily life, `靚仔[leng3zai2|handsome boy / plain white rice]` translates
literally to "handsome guy" or "handsome boy", and is often used to greet male
customers or friends. However, inside a
`茶餐廳[caa4caan1teng1|Hong Kong style café]`,
`靚仔[leng3zai2|handsome boy / plain white rice]` has a secondary
double-meaning: it is the universal restaurant slang for a **bowl of plain white
rice** (because it is pure white and clean).

To order a plate of char siu with white rice, you can naturally say:

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 一[jat1|one] 碟[dip6|plate (classifier)] 叉燒[caa1siu1|char siu] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 一[jat1|one] 碗[wun2|bowl] 靚仔[leng3zai2|handsome boy / plain white rice] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
===
I want a plate of barbecued pork and a bowl of plain white rice.
```

## 2. Completed Actions: Perfective Suffix `咗[zo2|completed action marker]`

In Cantonese, verbs do not change their form to express past tense. Instead, we
attach suffixes directly to the verb to express the **aspect** of the action
(how the action relates to time).

To express that an action has been successfully **completed**, attach the
perfective aspect suffix `咗[zo2|completed action marker]` (similar to Mandarin
_le_) directly behind the verb:

- `食[sik6|to eat]` + `咗[zo2|completed action marker]` (eaten / ate)
- `飲[jam2|drink]` + `咗[zo2|completed action marker]` (drunk / drank)

> [!IMPORTANT] **Word Order with `咗[zo2|completed action marker]`** When a verb
> takes an object, the perfective suffix `咗[zo2|completed action marker]` must
> stay firmly attached to the verb, coming _before_ the object. The structure is
> **Verb + `咗[zo2|completed action marker]` + Object**. For example, say
> `食[sik6|to eat]` `咗[zo2|completed action marker]` `嘢食[je5sik6|food]` (ate
> food), never `食[sik6|to eat]` `嘢食[je5sik6|food]`
> `咗[zo2|completed action marker]`.

### Stating Completed Events

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 飲[jam2|drink] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 咖啡[gaa3fe1|coffee] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！
===
I have drunk coffee!
```

```cantonese
佢哋[keoi5dei6|they] 食[sik6|to eat] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 菠蘿包[bo1lo1baau1|pineapple bun] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
===
They have eaten the pineapple buns.
```

### Asking "Have you yet?"

To ask if someone has completed an action yet, we place the adverb
`未[mei6|not yet]` at the end of the sentence, followed by the friendly question
particle
`呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]`:

```cantonese
你[nei5|you] 食[sik6|to eat] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 嘢食[je5sik6|food] 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
===
Have you eaten food yet?
```

To answer that you have not eaten yet, you can simply reply:

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 未[mei6|not yet] 食[sik6|to eat] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
===
I haven't eaten yet.
```

## 3. Basic Emotions & States

How do you feel during or after a long day? Here are some core adjectives to
describe your basic emotions and physical states:

- `開心[hoi1sam1|happy]`: Happy.
- `嬲[nau1|angry]`: Angry.
- `攰[gui6|tired]`: Tired.
- `悶[mun6|bored / boring]`: Bored.

### The Intensifier `好[hou2|good / very]` + Adjective

When `好[hou2|good / very]` is placed directly before another adjective, it acts
as an intensifier meaning "very". This is especially common when expressing
states or emotions.

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 好[hou2|good / very] 攰[gui6|tired] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
===
I am very tired.
```

```cantonese
佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 好[hou2|good / very] 開心[hoi1sam1|happy] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
===
He is very happy!
```

```cantonese
好[hou2|good / very] 靚[leng3|beautiful] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
===
It is very beautiful!
```

### Mini-Dialogue: Expressing Feelings

Here is a short exchange illustrating these new words.

```dialog
A: 你[nei5|you] 好[hou2|good / very] 嬲[nau1|angry] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Are you very angry?
B: 係[hai6|to be / yes] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]，我[ngo5|I] 好[hou2|good / very] 攰[gui6|tired] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 好[hou2|good / very] 悶[mun6|bored / boring] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。 我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 食[sik6|to eat] 嘢食[je5sik6|food]。
   === Yes, I am very tired and very bored. I want to eat food.
A: 你[nei5|you] 想[soeng2|to want to] 食[sik6|to eat] 蛋撻[daan6taat3|egg tart] 定[ding6|or] 菠蘿包[bo1lo1baau1|pineapple bun] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Do you want to eat an egg tart or a pineapple bun?
B: 我[ngo5|I] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 兩[loeng5|two] 個[go3|general classifier] 蛋撻[daan6taat3|egg tart] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！
   === I want two egg tarts!
A: 搞掂[gaau2dim6|done] 啦[laa3|sentence-final particle for change of state]！ 呢度[ni1dou6|here] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Done! Here it is.
B: 多謝[do1ze6|thank you]！ 我[ngo5|I] 而家[ji4gaa1|now] 好[hou2|good / very] 開心[hoi1sam1|happy] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
   === Thank you! I am very happy now!
```

## 4. Situational Dialogue: A Lively Café Lunch

Here is a realistic dialogue between a customer (A) and a busy diner waiter (B)
in a bustling Hong Kong `茶餐廳[caa4caan1teng1|Hong Kong style café]`.

```dialog
A: 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]！ 我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 一[jat1|one] 個[go3|general classifier] 菠蘿包[bo1lo1baau1|pineapple bun] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 一[jat1|one] 杯[bui1|cup] 奶茶[naai5caa4|milk tea] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Excuse me! I would like one pineapple bun and one cup of milk tea.
B: 好[hou2|good / very] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。 奶茶[naai5caa4|milk tea] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 熱[jit6|hot] 定[ding6|or] 凍[dung3|cold] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Sure. Do you want the milk tea hot or cold?
A: 我[ngo5|I] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 凍[dung3|cold] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle]。 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]！
   === I want the cold one. Thank you!
B: 好[hou2|good / very]，一[jat1|one] 個[go3|general classifier] 菠蘿包[bo1lo1baau1|pineapple bun]，一[jat1|one] 杯[bui1|cup] 凍[dung3|cold] 奶茶[naai5caa4|milk tea]。
   === Okay, one pineapple bun, one cup of cold milk tea.
A: 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]，我[ngo5|I] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle] 菠蘿包[bo1lo1baau1|pineapple bun] 搞掂[gaau2dim6|done] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Excuse me, is my pineapple bun ready yet?
B: 搞掂[gaau2dim6|done] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 啦[laa3|sentence-final particle for change of state]！ 呢度[ni1dou6|here] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]，唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]！
   === It is ready! Here it is, thank you!
A: 好[hou2|good / very] 靚[leng3|beautiful] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！ 多謝[do1ze6|thank you]！
   === It looks very beautiful! Thank you!
B: 你[nei5|you] 飲[jam2|drink] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 凍[dung3|cold] 奶茶[naai5caa4|milk tea] 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Have you drunk the cold milk tea yet?
A: 飲[jam2|drink] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 啦[laa3|sentence-final particle for change of state]，好飲[hou2jam2|delicious (for drinks)] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
   === Yes, I drank it, it is delicious!
A: 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]，我[ngo5|I]想[soeng2|to want to] 埋單[maai4daan1|to pay the bill] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。 幾多[gei2do1|how many / how much] 錢[cin2|money] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Excuse me, I want to pay the bill. How much is it?
B: 三十[saam1sap6|thirty] 蚊[man1|dollar] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]，唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]。
   === It is thirty dollars, thank you.
A: 好[hou2|good / very] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]，我[ngo5|I] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 現金[jin6gam1|cash] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === Great, I will pay with cash.
B: 搞掂[gaau2dim6|done] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！ 拜拜[baai1baai3|bye-bye]！
   === All done! Bye-bye!
A: 拜拜[baai1baai3|bye-bye]！
   === Bye-bye!
```
