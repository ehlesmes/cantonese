In this chapter, we will learn about **Mortgages, Loans & Getting Paid**.

Discussing salaries, paying bills, and managing loans is an essential part of
daily life in Hong Kong. We will learn standard and highly frequent colloquial
terms for these situations.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Mortgages, Loans & Targets

Here is the essential vocabulary for managing bills, loans, and salary in
Cantonese:

- `出糧[ceot1loeng4|salary]`: Getting paid your salary (literally "producing
  grain").
- `強積金[koeng5zik1gam1|MPF]`: Mandatory Provident Fund retirement scheme.
- `供樓[gung1lau2|to pay a mortgage]`: To pay a mortgage or make housing
  installments.
  - _Example_: `佢[keoi5|he / she / it]` `每[mui5|every]`
    `個[go3|general classifier]` `月[jyut6|month]` `要[jiu3|to want / need]`
    `俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let]` `兩[loeng5|two]`
    `萬[maan6|ten thousand]` `蚊[man1|dollar]`
    `供樓[gung1lau2|to pay a mortgage]`: He needs to pay twenty thousand dollars
    every month for the mortgage.
- `借[ze3|to borrow / lend]`: To borrow or lend. The direction depends on the
  structure:
  - `問[man6|to ask]` `人[jan4|person]` `借錢[ze3cin4|to borrow money]`: To
    borrow money _from_ someone.
  - `借錢[ze3cin4|to borrow money]` `俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let]`
    `人[jan4|person]`: To lend money _to_ someone.
- `還[waan4|to return]`: To return borrowed money or repay a loan.
- `跑數[paau2sou3|to hit a sales target]`: To chase sales targets or hit a
  financial quota (literally "running numbers").

## 2. Situational Dialogue: Mortgage and Loan Talk

Two friends discuss mortgages, salary payments, and loaning money.

```dialog
A: 你[nei5|you] 最近[zeoi3gan6|recently] 忙[mong4|busy] 緊[gan2|continuous aspect marker] 咩嘢[me1je5|what kind of thing] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === What have you been busy with recently?
B: 我[ngo5|I] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 供樓[gung1lau2|to pay a mortgage] 嘛[maa3|sentence-final particle indicating a statement is an obvious truth or reminder]，所以[so2ji5|therefore] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 努力[nou5lik6|to try hard] 跑數[paau2sou3|to hit a sales target] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]！
   === I need to pay a mortgage, so I have to work hard to hit my sales target, obviously!
A: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes]，依家[ji1gaa1|now] 供樓[gung1lau2|to pay a mortgage] 真係[zan1hai6|really] 貴[gwai3|expensive] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。好彩[hou2coi2|luckily] 每[mui5|every] 個[go3|general classifier] 月[jyut6|month] 出糧[ceot1loeng4|salary] 都[dou1|also / all] 幾[gei2|quite] 準時[zeon2si4|punctual] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle]。
   === Yes, paying a mortgage is really expensive now. Luckily, salary payments are quite punctual every month.
B: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes]，但係[daan6hai6|but] 扣除[kau3ceoi4|to deduct] 強積金[koeng5zik1gam1|MPF] 之後[zi1hau6|after]，都[dou1|also / all] 冇[mou5|to not have] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 一[jat1|one] 截[zit6|section] 喇[laa3|sentence-final particle for change of state]！
   === Indeed, but after deducting MPF, a chunk of it is gone already!
A: David 尋日[cam4jat6|yesterday] 問[man6|to ask] 我[ngo5|I] 借錢[ze3cin4|to borrow money] 添[tim1|particle indicating addition]，我[ngo5|I] 都[dou1|also / all] 唔[m4|not] 知[zi1|to know] 借唔借[ze3m4ze3|lend or not (A-not-A form)] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive) / let] 佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 好[hou2|good / very]。
   === David even asked to borrow money from me yesterday, I don't know whether I should lend it to him.
B: 佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 上次[soeng6ci3|last time] 借[ze3|to borrow / lend] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 錢[cin4|money] 都[dou1|also / all] 仲[zung6|still / yet] 未[mei6|not yet] 還[waan4|to return] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
   === He hasn't even repaid the money he borrowed last time!
```

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of mortgages, salaries, and loans in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Which Cantonese phrase refers to getting paid one's salary?
  A) 強積金[koeng5zik1gam1|MPF]
  B) 出糧[ceot1loeng4|salary]
  C) 供樓[gung1lau2|to pay a mortgage]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `出糧[ceot1loeng4|salary]` literally translates to "producing grain" and is the colloquial Cantonese term for getting paid one's salary.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blanks to say "to borrow money from someone" in Cantonese:
  ______ 人[jan4|person] ______。
answer: 問[man6|to ask] / 借錢[ze3cin4|to borrow money]
explanation: |
  To borrow money from someone, you use the structure `問[man6|to ask] 人[jan4|person] 借錢[ze3cin4|to borrow money]`.
```

explanation: |
  `佢[keoi5|he / she / it]` is "he", `每[mui5|every] 個[go3|general classifier] 月[jyut6|month]` is "every month", `要[jiu3|to want / need]` is "need to", `供樓[gung1lau2|to pay a mortgage]` is "pay mortgage", and `呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]` is the SFP.
