## 1. Internet & Smartphone Basics

In modern Hong Kong, technology is deeply integrated into daily life. Talking
about basic digital activities in colloquial Cantonese uses a blend of native
characters and highly integrated English loanwords:

- `上網[soeng5mong5|to go online / surf the internet]`: To go online, surf the
  internet, or connect to the web.
- `網上[mong5soeng6|online]`: Used as an adjective or adverb to describe online
  activities, games, or services.
- `下載[haa6zoi3|to download]`: To download files, videos, or applications.
- `智能電話[zi3nang4 din6waa2|smartphone]`: A smartphone.
- `智能[zi3nang4|smart / intelligent (tech)]`: Smart or intelligent when
  referring to modern technology.
- `WiFi[wai1faai1|Wi-Fi / wireless internet]`: Wi-Fi or wireless internet
  connection.
- `網絡[mong5lok6|internet / network]`: Internet connection or digital network.
- `網[mong5|internet / net / network]`: Short form for the internet or a
  network.

## 2. Apps, Messaging & Social Media Slang

Hong Kong social media and app culture has created incredibly colorful spoken
vocabulary. Many terms are direct Cantonese phonetics of English loanwords or
creative internet slang:

- `App[ep1|mobile application / app]`: Pronounced like the English "app" but
  with a classic Cantonese short, clipped tone `ep1`.
- `些牙[se1aa4|to share (loanword)]`: A highly popular loanword from English
  "share," meaning to share a link, photo, or post.
- `IG[ai1zi1|Instagram (slang)]`: Highly integrated colloquial term for
  Instagram.
- `FB[ef1bi1|Facebook (slang)]`: Colloquial term for Facebook.
- `WhatsApp[ep1si1ep1|WhatsApp / to WhatsApp someone]`: Refers to the WhatsApp
  messaging app, but is extremely common as a verb meaning "to WhatsApp
  someone." This verbalization applies to many app names in colloquial
  Cantonese.
  - _Example_: `你[nei5|you]` `陣間[zan6gaan1|later]`
    `WhatsApp[ep1si1ep1|WhatsApp]` `我[ngo5|me]` `啦[laa1|particle]`: WhatsApp
    me later.
- `傳[cyun4|to send / transmit / pass on]`: To send or transmit messages,
  photos, or files.
- `發送[faat3sung3|to send / transmit]`: To send or transmit.
- `覆[fuk1|to reply / write back]`: The standard spoken verb to reply to a text,
  email, or message.
- `留言[lau4jin4|to leave a message / comment]`: To leave a comment or message
  on a post.
- `短訊[dyun2seon3|text message / SMS]`: A text message or SMS.
- `錄音[luk6jam1|voice recording / to record audio]`: A voice note/message, or
  the act of recording audio.
- `片[pin2|video / movie / short clip]`: A video or short clip.
- `Cap圖[kep1tou4|to take a screenshot / screenshot (slang)]`: To take a
  screenshot (loanword from "capture").
- `已讀不回[ji5duk6bat1wui4|read without replying]`: Read without replying
  (leaving someone on read).
- `死機[sei2gei1|device freeze / crash]`: When a device freezes or crashes
  (literally "dead machine").

### Digital Payments

Hong Kong has rapidly adopted cashless payments, bringing new colloquial terms:

- `PayMe[pei1mi1|PayMe / to transfer money via PayMe]`: A ubiquitous local
  payment app. Like WhatsApp, it is frequently used as a verb.
  - _Example_: `我[ngo5|I]` `轉頭[zyun2tau4|later]` `PayMe[pei1mi1|PayMe]`
    `俾[bei2|to]` `你[nei5|you]`: I will PayMe you (transfer you money via
    PayMe) later.
- `轉數快[zyun3sou3faai3|FPS (Faster Payment System)]`: The government's instant
  inter-bank transfer system.
- `支付寶[zi1fu6bou2|Alipay]`: Alipay, another widely used digital wallet.

## 3. Smart Living & Tech Crises Slang

As smart home technology becomes more popular, terms like voice control are
common. Additionally, managing device batteries and data plans has birthed
standard spoken slangs:

- `智能家居[zi3nang4 gaa1geoi1|smart home]`: A smart home system.
- `智能鎖[zi3nang4 so2|smart lock]`: A smart lock.
- `冷氣[laang5hei3|air conditioning / AC]`: Air conditioning or AC.
- `開關[hoi1gwaan1|switch / to turn on or off]`: A switch, or to turn a device
  on or off.
- `聲控[sing1hung3|voice-controlled / voice control]`: Voice-controlled or using
  voice command.
- `熄[sik1|to turn off / extinguish (a light, device, etc.)]`: To turn off a
  device or extinguish a light.
- `燈[dang1|light / lamp]`: A light or lamp.
- `鎖[so2|to lock / lock]`: To lock something, or a lock itself.
- `火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter / charger (slang)]`: Literally "fire cow," the
  extremely popular local slang for a power adapter or wall charger.
- `尿袋[niu6doi2|portable power bank (slang)]`: Literally "urine bag," the
  classic, universal Hong Kong slang for a portable power bank.
- `充電[cung1din6|to charge a battery]`: To charge a battery.
- `數據[sou3geoi3|data]`: Mobile data or cellular data.
- `爆數據[baau3 sou3geoi3|to exceed mobile data limit (slang)]`: Literally
  "explode data," meaning to exceed your monthly mobile data plan limit.
- `密碼[mat6maa5|password]`: Password.
- `戶口[wu6hau2|account (bank/online)]`: A user account.
- `就快[zau6faai3|soon / about to]`: Soon, about to (often used before a verb,
  e.g., `就快爆數據[zau6 faai3 baau3 sou3 geoi3|about to exceed data limit]`).
- `百分之十[baak3fan6zi1sap6|ten percent]`: Ten percent.
- `插頭[caap3tau4|electrical socket / plug]`: Electrical socket, plug.
- `牆[coeng4|wall]`: Wall.
- `系統[hai6tung2|system]`: System.
- `大聲[daai6sing1|loudly / loud voice]`: Loudly, loud voice.

## 4. Situational Dialogues

### Dialogue 1: App Slang & Phone Battery Crisis

Ah Ming and Ah Wa are hanging out when Ah Ming's phone starts running out of
battery and data.

```dialog
A: 阿明[aa3 ming4|Ah Ming]，你[nei5|you] 睇[tai2|to look / see / watch / read] 咗[zo2|verbal aspect suffix indicating completed action (perfective aspect)] 我[ngo5|I] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] IG[ai1zi1|Instagram (slang)] 些牙[se1aa4|to share (loanword)] 俾[bei2|to give / for / let / allow] 你[nei5|you] 嗰[go2|that] 條[tiu4|measure word for long, thin things, also videos/messages] 片[pin2|video / movie / short clip] 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Ah Ming, have you watched that video I shared with you on Instagram yet?
B: 未[mei6|not yet] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！我[ngo5|I] 今日[gam1jat6|today] 唔[m4|not / no (negative prefix)] 敢[gam2|to dare] 用[jung6|to use] 智能電話[zi3nang4 din6waa2|smartphone] 上網[soeng5mong5|to go online / surf the internet] 睇[tai2|to look / see / watch / read] 片[pin2|video / movie / short clip]，因為[jan1wai6|because] 我[ngo5|I] 就快[zau6faai3|soon / quickly / about to] 爆數據[baau3 sou3geoi3|to exceed mobile data limit (slang)] 啦[laa1|particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！
   === Not yet! I don't dare use my smartphone to go online and watch videos today because I'm about to exceed my data limit!
A: 噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 你[nei5|you] 唔[m4|not / no (negative prefix)] 用[jung6|to use] 數據[sou3geoi3|data]，用[jung6|to use] 呢度[ni1dou6|here / this place] 嘅[ge3|possessive or linking particle] WiFi[wai1faai1|Wi-Fi / wireless internet] 咪[mai6|then / indeed (contraction of 唔係)] 得[dak1|particle expressing permission, possibility, capability, or adverbial degree] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle showing obviousness or exclamation]！
   === Then don't use your data, just use the Wi-Fi here!
B: 呢度[ni1dou6|here / this place] 嘅[ge3|possessive or linking particle] WiFi[wai1faai1|Wi-Fi / wireless internet] 要[jiu3|to want / need / require] 密碼[mat6maa5|password] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。同埋[tung4maai4|and / with / together with] 我[ngo5|I] 部[bou6|measure word for machines, phones, cars] 電話[din6waa2|telephone / phone] 得[dak1|particle expressing permission, possibility, capability, or adverbial degree] 返[faan1|to return] 百分之十[baak3fan6zi1sap6|ten percent] 嘅[ge3|possessive or linking particle] 電[din6|electricity / battery power] 咋[zaa3|only / that's all (particle limiting scope)]。你[nei5|you] 有冇[jau5mou5|is there / do you have (A-not-A question form)] 帶[daai3|to bring / carry] 尿袋[niu6doi2|portable power bank (slang)] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === The Wi-Fi here requires a password. And my phone only has ten percent battery left. Did you bring a power bank?
A: 我[ngo5|I] 冇[mou5|not have / there is not] 帶[daai3|to bring / carry] 尿袋[niu6doi2|portable power bank (slang)] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]，但係[daan6hai6|but / however] 我[ngo5|I] 有[jau5|to have / possess / there is / there are] 帶[daai3|to bring / carry] 火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter / charger (slang)]。你[nei5|you] 可以[ho2ji5|can / may / able to] 用[jung6|to use] 牆[coeng4|wall] 嗰個[go2go3|that one] 插頭[caap3tau4|electrical socket / plug] 充電[cung1din6|to charge a battery] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === I didn't bring a power bank, but I brought a power adapter. You can use that wall socket to charge your phone.
B: 太[taai3|too / extremely] 好[hou2|good / fine / very / quite] 啦[laa1|particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！等[dang2|to wait / wait for] 我[ngo5|I] 充[cung1|to charge / fill] 完[jyun4|finished / complete] 電[din6|electricity / battery power]，再[zoi3|again / furthermore] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] 網上[mong5soeng6|online] 下載[haa6zoi3|to download] 嗰[go2|that] 條[tiu4|measure word for long, thin things, also videos/messages] 片[pin2|video / movie / short clip] 睇[tai2|to look / see / watch / read] 啦[laa1|particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。你[nei5|you] 可唔可以[ho2m4ho2ji5|can or cannot / may or may not (A-not-A question form)] 先[sin1|first / in advance] WhatsApp[ep1si1ep1|WhatsApp / to WhatsApp someone] 個[go3|measure word for general items] 密碼[mat6maa5|password] 俾[bei2|to give / for / let / allow] 我[ngo5|I] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Great! Let me charge my phone first, and then I'll download that video online to watch. Can you WhatsApp the password to me first?
A: 冇問題[mou5 man6tai4|no problem / without issue]！我[ngo5|I] 依家[ji1gaa1|now] 傳[cyun4|to send / transmit / pass on] 俾[bei2|to give / for / let / allow] 你[nei5|you]，你[nei5|you] 記得[gei3dak1|to remember] 覆[fuk1|to reply / write back] 我[ngo5|I] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
   === No problem! I'll send it to you now. Remember to reply to me!
```

### Dialogue 2: Smart Living & Smart Home Tech

Ah Wa is visiting Ah Ming's new apartment, which is equipped with modern smart
home systems.

```dialog
A: 哇[waa1|wow]！阿明[aa3 ming4|Ah Ming]，你[nei5|you] 間[gaan1|measure word for rooms, houses, buildings] 新[san1|new] 屋[uk1|house / home] 好[hou2|good / fine / very / quite] 智能[zi3nang4|smart / intelligent (tech)] 喎[wo3|sentence-final particle indicating reported speech, surprise, or warning]！門口[mun4hau2|doorway / entrance] 用[jung6|to use] 智能鎖[zi3nang4 so2|smart lock] 嘅[ge3|possessive or linking particle] 咩[me1|rhetorical question particle expressing surprise or doubt]？
   === Wow! Ah Ming, your new home is so smart! Do you use a smart lock at the entrance?
B: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes]，我[ngo5|I] 依家[ji1gaa1|now] 唔使[m4sai2|no need / not necessary] 帶[daai3|to bring / carry] 鎖匙[so2si4|key / keys] 啦[laa1|particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。用[jung6|to use] 智能電話[zi3nang4 din6waa2|smartphone] 嘅[ge3|possessive or linking particle] App[ep1|mobile application / app] 就可以[zau6ho2ji5|then can / can easily] 鎖[so2|to lock / lock] 門[mun4|door] 或者[waak6ze2|perhaps / or] 開[hoi1|to write (a prescription) / prescribe / open / start] 門[mun4|door]，非常[fei1soeng4|very / extremely] 方便[fong1bin6|convenient]。
   === Yes, I don't need to carry keys now. I can easily lock or open the door using a smartphone app, it's extremely convenient.
A: 噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 入面[jap6min6|inside] 呢[ne1|particle for queries, gentle pause, or contrast]？有冇[jau5mou5|is there / do you have (A-not-A question form)] 智能家居[zi3nang4 gaa1geoi1|smart home] 系統[hai6tung2|system] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === What about inside? Do you have a smart home system?
B: 有[jau5|to have / possess / there is / there are] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！我[ngo5|I] 呢度[ni1dou6|here / this place] 係[hai6|to be] 聲控[sing1hung3|voice-controlled / voice control] 嘅[ge3|possessive or linking particle]。你[nei5|you] 試[si3|to try / test] 吓[haa5|verbal suffix indicating a brief action or attempt] 大聲[daai6sing1|loud voice / loudly] 講[gong2|to speak / say / talk] 「熄[sik1|to turn off / extinguish (a light, device, etc.)] 燈[dang1|light / lamp]」 啦[laa1|particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。

A: 熄[sik1|to turn off / extinguish (a light, device, etc.)] 燈[dang1|light / lamp]！咦[ji2|exclamation of surprise or sudden realization]！真係[zan1hai6|really / truly] 熄[sik1|to turn off / extinguish (a light, device, etc.)] 咗[zo2|verbal aspect suffix indicating completed action (perfective aspect)] 喎[wo3|sentence-final particle indicating reported speech, surprise, or warning]！噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 點樣[dim2joeng6|how / in what way] 開[hoi1|to write (a prescription) / prescribe / open / start] 返[faan1|to return] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Turn off light! Oh! It really turned off! Then how do you turn it back on?
B: 你[nei5|you] 可以[ho2ji5|can / may / able to] 按[on3|to press / push / according to] 呢個[ni1go3|this one] 開關[hoi1gwaan1|switch / to turn on or off]，或者[waak6ze2|perhaps / or] 講[gong2|to speak / say / talk] 「開[hoi1|to write (a prescription) / prescribe / open / start] 燈[dang1|light / lamp]」 就可以[zau6ho2ji5|then can / can easily] 啦[laa1|particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。

   === Wow! Smart homes are really awesome!
```

## 5. Exercises

Test your understanding of modern tech, apps, and smart living slang in
Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following local slangs refers to a "portable power bank" in Hong Kong Cantonese?
  A) 火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter / charger (slang)]
  B) 尿袋[niu6doi2|portable power bank (slang)]
  C) 智能電話[zi3nang4 din6waa2|smartphone]
  D) 智能鎖[zi3nang4 so2|smart lock]
answer: B
explanation: 尿袋[niu6doi2|portable power bank (slang)] (literally "urine bag") is the ubiquitous Hong Kong slang for a portable power bank. 火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter / charger (slang)] is the slang for a wall adapter/charger.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb for "to reply" or "to write back":
  我[ngo5|I] 依家[ji1gaa1|now] 傳[cyun4|to send / transmit / pass on] 密碼[mat6maa5|password] 俾[bei2|to give / for / let / allow] 你[nei5|you]，你[nei5|you] 記得[gei3dak1|to remember] ______ 我[ngo5|I] 呀[aa3|particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
answer: 覆[fuk1|to reply / write back]
explanation: 覆[fuk1|to reply / write back] is the standard colloquial verb used to ask someone to reply to a message or email.
```
