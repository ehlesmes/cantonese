## 1. Smart Home & Tech Crises Slang

Managing your home systems, electrical sockets, and device charges involves
unique terminology:

- `智能家居[zi3nang4 gaa1geoi1|smart home]`: Smart home systems.
- `智能鎖[zi3nang4 so2|smart lock]`: A smart lock.
- `冷氣[laang5hei3|air conditioning / AC]`: Air conditioning or AC.
- `開關[hoi1gwaan1|switch / to turn on or off]`: A switch, or to turn on/off.
- `聲控[sing1hung3|voice-controlled / voice control]`: Voice-controlled.
- `熄[sik1|to turn off / extinguish (a light, device, etc.)]`: To turn off a
  device/light.
- `燈[dang1|light / lamp]`: A light or lamp.
- `鎖[so2|to lock / lock]`: To lock, or a lock itself.
- `火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter / charger (slang)]`: Literally "fire cow," the
  popular slang for a power adapter/wall charger.
- `尿袋[niu6doi2|portable power bank (slang)]`: Literally "urine bag," the slang
  for a portable power bank.
- `充電[cung1din6|to charge a battery]`: To charge a battery.
- `數據[sou3geoi3|data]`: Mobile data.
- `爆數據[baau3 sou3geoi3|to exceed mobile data limit (slang)]`: Exceeding
  mobile data plans.
- `密碼[mat6maa5|password]`: Password.
- `戶口[wu6hau2|account (bank/online)]`: User account.
- `就快[zau6faai3|soon / about to]`: Soon, about to.
- `百分之十[baak3fan1zi1sap6|ten percent]`: Ten percent.
- `插頭[caap3tau4|electrical socket / plug]`: Electrical socket or plug.
- `牆[coeng4|wall]`: Wall.
- `系統[hai6tung2|system]`: System.
- `大聲[daai6sing1|loudly / loud voice]`: Loudly.

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of smart home terminology in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following refers to a portable power bank in Hong Kong slang?
  A) 火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter]
  B) 智能鎖[zi3nang4 so2|smart lock]
  C) 尿袋[niu6doi2|power bank]
  D) 系統[hai6tung2|system]
answer: C
explanation: |
  `尿袋[niu6doi2|power bank]` is the local slang for a portable power bank, while `火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter]` is a power adapter.
```

```exercise
question: |
  What is the slang term `火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter]` literally translated as?
  A) Portable power bank
  B) Fire cow
  C) Smart lock
answer: B
explanation: |
  `火牛[fo2ngau4|power adapter]` literally translates to "fire cow" and refers to a wall power adapter.
```

```exercise

  "I want to turn off the light."

explanation: |
  `我[ngo5|I / me]` is "I", `想[soeng2|to want to]` is "want to", `熄[sik1|to turn off]` is "to turn off", `燈[dang1|light]` is "light", and `呀[aa3|particle]` is the SFP.
```
