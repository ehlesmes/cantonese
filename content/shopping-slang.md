## 1. Authentic Hong Kong Transactions

When buying something at a local store, you will encounter terms for local
payment methods, change, and convenience items.

### Payment Methods: Octopus & Cash

Hong Kong is famous for its contactless smart card, the
`八達通[baat3daat6tung1|Octopus card]`. Alternatively, you can use
`現金[jin6gam1|cash]`.

To ask if a store accepts a payment method, use the verb `收[sau1|to receive]`
in the A-not-A question form `收唔收[sau1 m4 sau1|do you accept?]`:

```cantonese
呢度[ni1dou6|here] 收唔收[sau1 m4 sau1|do you accept?] 八達通[baat3daat6tung1|Octopus card] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
===
Do you accept Octopus card here?
```

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 冇[mou5|to not have] 現金[jin6gam1|cash] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
===
I don't have cash.
```

### Plastic Bags & Change

At the register, the clerk will often ask if you need a
`膠袋[gaau1doi2|plastic bag]`:

```cantonese
要唔要[jiu3 m4 jiu3|do you want?] 膠袋[gaau1doi2|plastic bag] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
===
Do you want a plastic bag?
```

If you pay with cash, the clerk will use the verb
`找[zaau2|to seek / give change]` to give you change:

```cantonese
找[zaau2|to seek / give change] 十[sap6|ten] 蚊[man1|dollar]。
===
Here is ten dollars change.
```

## 2. Daily Slang & Loanwords

### The Ultimate Slang: `搞掂[gaau2dim6|done]`

One of the most essential Cantonese colloquial words is `搞掂[gaau2dim6|done]`.
It is an incredibly versatile adjective/expression used to declare that a task
is finished, a problem is solved, or a transaction is successfully completed.

- When you finish paying: `搞掂[gaau2dim6|done]`！ (Done! / All settled!)
- When a task is complete: `搞掂[gaau2dim6|done]`
  `啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]`！ (Sorted!)

### English Loanwords

Spoken Cantonese naturally absorbs English words, adapting their pronunciation
to fit Cantonese phonetics. Here are three crucial loanwords:

- `的士[dik1si2|taxi]`: Taxi
- `巴士[baa1si2|bus]`: Bus
- `飛[fei1|ticket (loanword, e.g., movie ticket, bus ticket)]`: Ticket

```cantonese
我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 買[maai5|buy] 巴士[baa1si2|bus] 飛[fei1|ticket (loanword, e.g., movie ticket, bus ticket)] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
===
I want to buy a bus ticket.
```

```cantonese
呢度[ni1dou6|here] 冇[mou5|to not have] 的士[dik1si2|taxi] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
===
There are no taxis here.
```

## 3. Conversational Dialogue

> [!NOTE] **`唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]` vs `多謝[do1ze6|thank you]`**
> Cantonese has two distinct ways to say "thank you." Use
> `唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]` to thank someone for a service or help (like
> a clerk handing you an item). Use `多謝[do1ze6|thank you]` when receiving a
> gift, paying, or in a retail context where the cashier thanks you for your
> business.

Below is a realistic interaction between a customer (A) and a convenience store
clerk (B) in Hong Kong.

```dialog
A: 你好[nei5hou2|hello]呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！ 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]，我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 買[maai5|buy] 呢個[ni1go3|this one] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 嗰個[go2go3|that one]。
   === Hello there! Excuse me, I want to buy this one and that one.
B: 好[hou2|good / very] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。 呢個[ni1go3|this one] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 可樂[ho2lok6|cola]，嗰個[go2go3|that one] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 薯片[syu4pin2|potato chips]。
   === Sure. This one is Coke, that one is potato chips.
A: 幾多[gei2do1|how many / how much] 錢[cin2|money] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === How much is it?
B: 三十[saam1sap6|thirty] 蚊[man1|dollar] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。 要唔要[jiu3 m4 jiu3|do you want?] 膠袋[gaau1doi2|plastic bag] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === It is thirty dollars. Do you want a plastic bag?
A: 唔[m4|not] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]，唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]。 呢度[ni1dou6|here] 收唔收[sau1 m4 sau1|do you accept?] 八達通[baat3daat6tung1|Octopus card] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === No, thank you. Do you accept Octopus card here?
B: 收[sau1|to receive] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！ 多謝[do1ze6|thank you]。
   === Yes, we do! Thank you.
A: 好[hou2|good / very] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。 仲有[zung6jau5|furthermore]，我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 買[maai5|buy] 巴士[baa1si2|bus] 飛[fei1|ticket (loanword, e.g., movie ticket, bus ticket)] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Okay. Also, I want to buy a bus ticket.
B: 冇[mou5|to not have] 問題[man6tai4|problem / question]！ 搞掂[gaau2dim6|done] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！ 多謝[do1ze6|thank you]！
   === No problem! All done! Thank you!
A: 唔該[m4goi1|thank you / please]！ 拜拜[baai1baai3|bye-bye]！
   === Thanks! Bye-bye!
B: 拜拜[baai1baai3|bye-bye]！
   === Bye-bye!
```
