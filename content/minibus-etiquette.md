## 1. Minibus Shouting Etiquette

Riding a minibus (`紅[hung4|red]` `VAN[man1|minibus (loanword)]` or
`綠[luk6|green]` `VAN[man1|minibus (loanword)]`) is a quintessential Hong Kong
experience. Unlike double-decker buses, minibuses do not have stop buttons or
automated announcements. Instead, passengers must shout out to the
`司機[si1gei1|driver]` when they want to request a stop.

### The Stop Request Template

To request a stop, use this formula:

- `唔該[m4goi1|please]` + Landmark/Location + `有落[jau5lok6|stop here please]`

### Common Stop Request Expressions

Here are the most common phrases passengers shout on a minibus:

- `唔該[m4goi1|please]` `前邊[cin4bin1|ahead]`
  `有落[jau5lok6|stop here please]`: Stop up ahead, please.
- `唔該[m4goi1|please]` `路口[lou6hau2|intersection]`
  `有落[jau5lok6|stop here please]`: Stop at the intersection, please.
- `唔該[m4goi1|please]` `燈位[dang1wai2|traffic light]`
  `有落[jau5lok6|stop here please]`: Stop at the traffic light, please.
- `唔該[m4goi1|please]` `地鐵站[dei6tit3zaam6|MTR station / subway station]`
  `有落[jau5lok6|stop here please]`: Stop at the MTR station, please.

To get on a vehicle, use `上車[soeng5ce1|to get on a vehicle]`, and to get off,
use `落車[lok6ce1|to get off a vehicle]`.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: Riding a Red Minibus

A passenger (A) is trying to get to the subway station quickly and coordinates
with the minibus driver (B).

```dialog
A: 唔該[m4goi1|please]，我[ngo5|I / me] 趕時間[gon2si4gaan3|in a hurry / rushing for time]，請問[cing2man6|may I ask] 架[gaa3|general measure word / individual / unit / classifier for vehicles] 車[ce1|car / vehicle] 去[heoi3|to go] 地鐵站[dei6tit3zaam6|MTR station / subway station] 要[jiu3|need] 幾耐[gei2noi6|how long (time)] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Excuse me, I am in a hurry. May I ask how long it takes for this vehicle to get to the MTR station?
B: 前邊[cin4bin1|ahead / in front / up ahead] 塞車[sak1ce1|traffic jam / heavy traffic] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]，大約[daai6joek3|approximately] 十[sap6|ten] 分鐘[fan1zung1|minutes] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === There is a traffic jam up ahead, about ten minutes.
A: 噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 我[ngo5|I / me] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] 前邊[cin4bin1|ahead / in front / up ahead] 天橋[tin1kiu4|pedestrian flyover / footbridge] 落車[lok6ce1|to get off a vehicle] 行路[haang4lou6|to walk] 過去[gwo3heoi3|to go over] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。唔該[m4goi1|please] 司機[si1gei1|driver]，天橋[tin1kiu4|pedestrian flyover / footbridge] 有落[jau5lok6|stop here please]！
   === Then I will get off at the pedestrian flyover ahead and walk over. Thanks, driver. Please stop at the pedestrian flyover!
B: 收到[sau1dou2|got it]！呢度[ni1dou6|here] 唔[m4|not / no (negative prefix)] 可以[ho2ji5|can / may / possible] 落車[lok6ce1|to get off a vehicle]，過[gwo3|to pass / cross] 咗[zo2|verbal aspect suffix indicating completed action (perfective aspect)] 燈位[dang1wai2|traffic light / intersection with lights] 先[sin1|first / only then] 俾[bei2|to give / by (passive marker) / let] 你[nei5|you] 落[lok6|to descend / fall / drop / get off] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]。
   === Got it! I cannot let you off here. I will let you off after we pass the traffic light.
```
