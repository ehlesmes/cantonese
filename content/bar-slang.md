In this chapter, we will learn about **Bars & Nightlife Slang**.

Hong Kong's nightlife and social scene is incredibly vibrant. To describe
activities like going to bars, singing karaoke, or hanging out late at night,
Cantonese speakers use highly expressive colloquial terms.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Socializing & Night-Out Habits

Here are the key terms for navigating Hong Kong's nightlife hotspots:

- `唱[coeng3|to sing]`K: An extremely popular pastime. The letter "K" is a
  shorthand loanword for karaoke (pronounced `coeng3 kei1`). Because it is a
  separable verb, aspect markers can be inserted in the middle.
  - _Example_: `我[ngo5|I / me]` `想[soeng2|to want to / would like to]`
    `去[heoi3|to go]` `唱[coeng3|to sing]` `咗[zo2|completed]` `K[kei1|karaoke]`
    `啦[laa1|particle]`！: I've gone and sung karaoke already!
- `卡拉[kaa1laa1|karaoke]`OK: The full standard term for karaoke (pronounced
  `kaa1 laa1 ou1 kei1`).
- `蒲[pou4|to hang out / go clubbing]`: A classic slang term meaning to hang out
  or go clubbing, typically in nightlife hotspots.
- `夜蒲[je6pou4|night clubbing / nightlife / to club at night]`: To go clubbing
  or participate in late-night entertainment.
- `酒吧[zau2baa1|bar / pub]`: A bar, pub, or lounge.
- `飲酒[jam2zau2|to drink alcohol / have a drink]`: To drink alcohol or have a
  social drink.
- `劈酒[pek3zau2|to drink heavily / drink competitively]`: To down drinks,
  engage in heavy drinking, or play drinking games at a bar.
- `醉[zeoi3|drunk]`: Intoxicated or drunk.
- `斷片[dyun6pin2|to blackout / memory lapse from drinking]`: To blackout or
  suffer a memory lapse from excessive drinking.
- `琴晚[kam4maan5|last night]`: Last night.
- `晏[aan3|late]`: Late (referring to the time of day, e.g.,
  `幾晏[gei2aan3|quite late]`).
- `或者[waak6ze2|perhaps / or]`: Or, perhaps.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: The Night Out at the Bar

Sau-han (A) and Wing-kit (B) catch up after Friday night.

```dialog
A: 琴晚[kam4maan5|last night] 我哋[ngo5dei6|we / us] 去[heoi3|to go] 酒吧[zau2baa1|bar / pub] 夜蒲[je6pou4|night clubbing / nightlife / to club at night]，真係[zan1hai6|really / truly] 玩[waan2|to play / have fun / enjoy] 得[dak1|particle expressing permission, possibility, capability, or adverbial degree] 好[hou2|very / quite / extremely] 開心[hoi1sam1|happy / glad / cheerful] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
   === Last night we went clubbing at a bar, we really had a very fun time!
B: 係咩[hai6me1|really? / is that so?]？你哋[nei5dei6|you (plural)] 有冇[jau5mou5|is there / do you have (A-not-A question form)] 飲酒[jam2zau2|to drink alcohol / have a drink] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Really? Did you guys drink?
A: 有呀[jau5aa3|yes]！我哋[ngo5dei6|we / us] 仲[zung6|still / also / even more] 劈酒[pek3zau2|to drink heavily / drink competitively] 添[tim1|as well / also / particle]！David 飲[jam2|to drink] 得[dak1|particle expressing permission, possibility, capability, or adverbial degree] 太多[taai3do1|too much]，最後[zeoi3hau6|finally / at last / in the end] 醉[zeoi3|drunk] 咗[zo2|verbal aspect suffix indicating completed action (perfective aspect)]，仲[zung6|still / also / even more] 斷片[dyun6pin2|to blackout / memory lapse from drinking] 添[tim1|as well / also / particle]！
   === Yes! We even drank heavily! David drank too much, ended up drunk, and even blacked out!
B: 哇[waa1|wow / whoa]！噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 今日[gam1jat6|today] 係唔係[hai6m4hai6|is it or not? / is that right? (A-not-A question form)] 覺得[gok3dak1|to feel / think] 鬼死咁[gwai2sei2gam3|extremely / awfully (pre-adjective intensifier)] 攰[gui6|tired / fatigued] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Wow! Then does he feel extremely exhausted today?
A: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes]，佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 今日[gam1jat6|today] 只係[zi2hai6|only / just] 想[soeng2|to want to / would like to / think] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] 屋企[uk1kei2|home] 聽歌[teng1go1|to listen to music] 同埋[tung4maai4|and / together with] 睇書[tai2syu1|to read books]，邊度[bin1dou6|where] 都[dou1|also / even / already] 唔[m4|not / no (negative prefix)] 想[soeng2|to want to / would like to / think] 去[heoi3|to go] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Yes, today he only wants to listen to music and read books at home, he doesn't want to go anywhere.
```

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of bar slang and karaoke terms in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following terms means "to drink heavily" or "drink competitively"?
  A) 蒲[pou4|to hang out]
  B) 劈酒[pek3zau2|to drink heavily]
  C) 斷片[dyun6pin2|to blackout]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `劈酒[pek3zau2|to drink heavily / drink competitively]` is the colloquial slang for heavy drinking or drinking games at a bar.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb for "to sing karaoke":
  今晚[gam1maan5|tonight] 我哋[ngo5dei6|we / us] 一齊[jat1cai4|together] 去[heoi3|to go] ______ 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle showing obviousness, resignation, or 'of course']。
answer: 唱[coeng3|to sing]K
explanation: |
  `唱[coeng3|to sing]K` is the highly colloquial and standard spoken Cantonese verb for singing karaoke.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Translate the following sentence into colloquial Cantonese:
  "Last night he drank too much and blacked out."

explanation: |
  `佢[keoi5|he / she / it]` is "he", `琴晚[kam4maan5|last night]` is "last night", `飲[jam2|to drink] 得[dak1|particle expressing permission, possibility, capability, or adverbial degree] 太多[taai3do1|too much]` means "drank too much", `斷片[dyun6pin2|to blackout]` means "blacked out", and `咗[zo2|aspect particle]` is the completion marker.
```
