In this chapter, we will learn about **Bars & Nightlife Slang**.

Hong Kong's nightlife and social scene is incredibly vibrant. To describe
activities like going to bars, singing karaoke, or hanging out late at night,
Cantonese speakers use highly expressive colloquial terms.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Socializing & Night-Out Habits

Here are the key terms for navigating Hong Kong's nightlife hotspots:

- `唱[coeng3|to sing]`K: An extremely popular pastime. The letter "K" is a
  shorthand loanword for karaoke (pronounced `coeng3 kei1`). Because it is a
  separable verb, aspect markers can be inserted in the middle.
  - _Example_: `我[ngo5|I]` `想[soeng2|to want to]` `去[heoi3|go]`
    `唱[coeng3|to sing]` `咗[zo2|completed action marker]` `K[kei1|karaoke]`
    `啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]`！:
    I've gone and sung karaoke already!
- `卡拉[kaa1laa1|karaoke]`OK: The full standard term for karaoke (pronounced
  `kaa1 laa1 ou1 kei1`).
- `蒲[pou4|to go clubbing]`: A classic slang term meaning to hang out or go
  clubbing, typically in nightlife hotspots.
- `夜蒲[je6pou4|night clubbing]`: To go clubbing or participate in late-night
  entertainment.
- `酒吧[zau2baa1|bar]`: A bar, pub, or lounge.
- `飲酒[jam2zau2|to drink alcohol]`: To drink alcohol or have a social drink.
- `劈酒[pek3zau2|to drink heavily]`: To down drinks, engage in heavy drinking,
  or play drinking games at a bar.
- `醉[zeoi3|drunk]`: Intoxicated or drunk.
- `斷片[dyun6pin2|to blackout]`: To blackout or suffer a memory lapse from
  excessive drinking.
- `琴晚[kam4maan5|last night]`: Last night.
- `晏[aan3|late]`: Late (referring to the time of day, e.g.,
  `幾晏[gei2aan3|quite late]`).
- `或者[waak6ze2|perhaps / or]`: Or, perhaps.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: The Night Out at the Bar

Sau-han (A) and Wing-kit (B) catch up after Friday night.

```dialog
A: 琴晚[kam4maan5|last night] 我哋[ngo5dei6|we] 去[heoi3|go] 酒吧[zau2baa1|bar] 夜蒲[je6pou4|night clubbing]，真係[zan1hai6|really] 玩[waan2|to play] 得[dak1|okay / can / ready] 好[hou2|good / very] 開心[hoi1sam1|happy] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
   === Last night we went clubbing at a bar, we really had a very fun time!
B: 係咩[hai6me1|really?]？你哋[nei5dei6|you (plural)] 有冇[jau5mou5|do you have?] 飲酒[jam2zau2|to drink alcohol] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Really? Did you guys drink?
A: 有呀[jau5aa3|yes]！我哋[ngo5dei6|we] 仲[zung6|still / yet] 劈酒[pek3zau2|to drink heavily] 添[tim1|particle indicating addition]！David 飲[jam2|drink] 得[dak1|okay / can / ready] 太多[taai3do1|too much]，最後[zeoi3hau6|finally] 醉[zeoi3|drunk] 咗[zo2|completed action marker]，仲[zung6|still / yet] 斷片[dyun6pin2|to blackout] 添[tim1|particle indicating addition]！
   === Yes! We even drank heavily! David drank too much, ended up drunk, and even blacked out!
B: 哇[waa1|wow]！噉[gam2|like this / then] 佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 今日[gam1jat6|today] 係唔係[hai6m4hai6|is it?] 覺得[gok3dak1|to feel / think] 鬼死咁[gwai2sei2gam3|awfully] 攰[gui6|tired] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Wow! Then does he feel extremely exhausted today?
A: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes]，佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 今日[gam1jat6|today] 只係[zi2hai6|only] 想[soeng2|to want to] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] 屋企[uk1kei2|home] 聽歌[teng1go1|to listen to music] 同埋[tung4maai4|and] 睇書[tai2syu1|to read books]，邊度[bin1dou6|where] 都[dou1|also / all] 唔[m4|not] 想[soeng2|to want to] 去[heoi3|go] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === Yes, today he only wants to listen to music and read books at home, he doesn't want to go anywhere.
```

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of bar slang and karaoke terms in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Which of the following terms means "to drink heavily" or "drink competitively"?
  A) 蒲[pou4|to go clubbing]
  B) 劈酒[pek3zau2|to drink heavily]
  C) 斷片[dyun6pin2|to blackout]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `劈酒[pek3zau2|to drink heavily]` is the colloquial slang for heavy drinking or drinking games at a bar.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb for "to sing karaoke":
  今晚[gam1maan5|tonight] 我哋[ngo5dei6|we] 一齊[jat1cai4|together] 去[heoi3|go] ______ 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]。
answer: 唱[coeng3|to sing]K
explanation: |
  `唱[coeng3|to sing]K` is the highly colloquial and standard spoken Cantonese verb for singing karaoke.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Translate the following sentence into colloquial Cantonese:
  "Last night he drank too much and blacked out."
answer: 佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 琴晚[kam4maan5|last night] 飲[jam2|drink] 得[dak1|okay / can / ready] 太多[taai3do1|too much] 斷[dyun6|to snap] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 片[pin2|video / movie] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
explanation: |
  `佢[keoi5|he / she / it]` is "he", `琴晚[kam4maan5|last night]` is "last night", `飲[jam2|drink] 得[dak1|okay / can / ready] 太多[taai3do1|too much]` means "drank too much", `斷片[dyun6pin2|to blackout]` means "blacked out", and `咗[zo2|completed action marker]` is the completion marker.
```
