## 1. Types of Housing in Hong Kong

Hong Kong’s high-density urban environment has led to unique residential
structures. When navigating the housing market, it is essential to distinguish
between traditional buildings and modern developments:

- `唐樓[tong4lau2|traditional low-rise walk-up building]`: These are older
  low-rise buildings (typically 5 to 8 stories) constructed without a
  `升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator / lift]`. They are famous for their high
  ceilings and historical value, though climbing them can be physically
  demanding.
- `洋樓[joeng4lau2|modern high-rise building with elevator]`: These are modern
  mid-to-high-rise residential buildings equipped with a
  `升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator / lift]` and usually have a security guard at
  the lobby entrance.
- `套房[tou3fong2|studio flat / ensuite bedroom]`: A studio apartment or a
  single self-contained bedroom containing its own private bathroom, and
  sometimes a small kitchenette.
- `劏房[tong1fong2|subdivided flat / partitioned room]`: A flat that has been
  partitioned or split into multiple tiny individual units. These are highly
  compact and common in older urban areas.
- `公屋[gung1uk1|public housing]`: Public housing, which houses a massive
  portion of the population.
- `屋[uk1|house / room]`: A general term for a house or room.
- `樓[lau2|building / flat / apartment / floor]`: A generic term that can mean a
  building, a flat, an apartment, or a floor.

## 2. Flat-Hunting & Rental Terminology

The process of finding and securing a place to live involves specific
terminology:

- `搵樓[wan2lau2|to look for apartments / flat-hunting]`: The general action of
  flat-hunting or searching for an apartment.
- `睇樓[tai2lau2|to view apartments / flats]`: Going to view apartments or flats
  in person with an agent or landlord.
- `租[zou1|to rent / lease]`: The verb meaning to rent or to lease a property.
- `租客[zou1haak3|tenant / renter]`: The tenant or renter who leases the
  property.
- `業主[jip6zyu2|landlord / property owner]`: The property owner or landlord.
- `租金[zou1gam1|rent payment / rental price]`: The monthly rental price or
  payment.
- `按金[on3gam1|deposit]`: The security deposit held by the landlord.
- `上期[soeng5kei4|rent paid in advance (first month rent)]`: The first month of
  rent paid in advance.
- `兩按一上[loeng5on3jat1soeng5|two months deposit and one month rent in advance]`:
  The standard Hong Kong upfront rental payment scheme consisting of two months
  of security deposit and one month of advance rent.
- `合約[hap6joek3|contract / agreement]`: The formal lease contract or
  agreement.
- `佣金[jung2gam1|commission fee]`: The agency commission fee, usually split
  between the tenant and landlord.
- `屋苑[uk1jyun2|housing estate / complex]`: A housing estate or residential
  complex.
- `管理費[gun2lei5fai3|management fee]`: The building management fee.

## 3. Apartment Layouts & Neighborhood Life

To describe your living space and neighborhood, use these vocabulary words:

- `客廳[haak3ting1|living room / parlor]`: The main living or sitting room.
- `睡房[seoi6fong2|bedroom]`: The bedroom.
- `面積[min6zik1|area / size]`: The total size or area of a flat.
- `實用面積[sat6jung6 min6zik1|saleable area / net floor area]`: The net
  saleable area of the apartment (excluding common areas and thick walls).
- `呎[cek3|square foot / feet]`: The local standard unit of area measurement,
  shorthand for square feet.

_Example Layout Description:_

- `間[gaan1|measure word for rooms]` `屋[uk1|house/flat]` `有[jau5|has]`
  `兩[loeng5|two]` `間[gaan1|measure word]` `睡房[seoi6fong2|bedroom]`
  `同[tung4|and]` `一[jat1|one]` `個[go3|measure word]`
  `客廳[haak3ting1|living room]`，`實用面積[sat6jung6min6zik1|saleable area]`
  `五百[ng5baak3|five hundred]` `呎[cek3|square feet]`: The flat has two
  bedrooms and one living room, with a saleable area of five hundred square
  feet.
- `環境[waan4ging2|environment / surroundings]`: The local surroundings or
  environmental quality of a neighborhood.
- `方便[fong1bin6|convenient]`: Describing high ease of access to transit,
  shops, or facilities.
- `周圍[zau1wai4|around / surroundings]`: The surrounding area around the
  building.
- `嘈[cou4|noisy]`: Noisy or loud surroundings.
- `靜[zing6|quiet / peaceful]`: Quiet, calm, or peaceful surroundings.
- `好住[hou2zyu6|nice to live in / comfortable to reside]`: Comfortable, cozy,
  and highly pleasant to reside in. This uses the grammatical structure of
  `好[hou2|good]` + Verb (`住[zyu6|to live]`) to create an adjective meaning
  "good to [verb]" or "easy to [verb]".
  - _Example_: `呢個[ni1go3|this]` `屋苑[uk1jyun2|housing estate]`
    `真係[zan1hai6|really]` `好住[hou2zyu6|nice to live in]`！: This housing
    estate is really nice to live in!
- `附近[fu6gan6|nearby / neighborhood]`: Nearby or adjacent to a location.
- `最好[zeoi3hou2|best / preferably]`: Best or preferably.

## 4. Situational Dialogues

### Dialogue 1: Finding an Apartment with an Agent

A client (`A`) meets with a real estate agent (`B`) to discuss their
requirements and view apartments.

```dialog
A: 唔該[m4goi1|please]，我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|want to] 搵樓[wan2lau2|to look for apartments / flat-hunting]。我[ngo5|I] 嘅[ge3|possessive particle] 要求[jiu1kau4|to require / requirement] 係[hai6|to be] 附近[fu6gan6|nearby / neighborhood] 要[jiu3|need] 方便[fong1bin6|convenient]，最好[zeoi3hou2|best / preferably] 有[jau5|have] 升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator / lift] 嘅[ge3|associative particle] 洋樓[joeng4lau2|modern high-rise building with elevator]。
   === Excuse me, I want to look for apartments. My requirement is that the neighborhood must be convenient, preferably a modern high-rise building with an elevator.
B: 冇問題[mou5 man6tai4|no problem / without issue]！呢個[ni1go3|this one] 地段[dei6dyun6|location / sector] 有[jau5|have] 個[go3|measure word] 單位[daan1wai2|unit / flat / apartment]。佢[keoi5|he / she] 有[jau5|have] 一[jat1|one] 個[go3|measure word] 客廳[haak3ting1|living room / parlor] 同埋[tung4maai4|and / with / together with] 一[jat1|one] 間[gaan1|classifier for buildings / shops / rooms] 睡房[seoi6fong2|bedroom]，實用面積[sat6jung6 min6zik1|saleable area / net floor area] 四[sei3|four] 百[baak3|hundred] 呎[cek3|square foot / feet] 啦[laa1|particle]。
   === No problem! There is a unit in this location. It has one living room and one bedroom, with a saleable area of about four hundred square feet.
A: 好似[hou2ci5|seem / seem like / as if] 幾[gei2|quite / pretty / fairly] 好[hou2|good / fine / very / quite] 喎[wo3|particle]！租金[zou1gam1|rent payment / rental price] 幾多錢[gei2 do1 cin2|how much money] 呀[aa3|particle]？
   === That sounds pretty good! How much is the rent?
B: 租金[zou1gam1|rent payment / rental price] 一[jat1|one] 萬[maan6|ten thousand] 五[ng5|five] 千[cin1|thousand] 蚊[man1|dollar] 一[jat1|one] 個[go3|measure word] 月[jyut6|month]，要[jiu3|need] 兩按一上[loeng5on3jat1soeng5|two months deposit and one month rent in advance]，同埋[tung4maai4|and / with / together with] 簽約[cim1joek3|to sign a contract] 嘅[ge3|possessive particle] 時候[si4hau6|time / when] 俾[bei2|to give] 半[bun3|half] 個[go3|measure word] 租金[zou1gam1|rent payment / rental price] 做[zou6|to do / make / perform] 佣金[jung2gam1|commission fee] 啦[laa1|particle]。
   === The rent is fifteen thousand dollars a month. It requires two months deposit and one month rent in advance, and when signing the contract, you pay half a month's rent as a commission fee.
A: 噉[gam2|then] 我哋[ngo5dei6|we / us] 幾時[gei2si4|when / what time] 可以[ho2ji5|can] 去[heoi3|to go] 睇樓[tai2lau2|to view apartments / flats] 呀[aa3|particle]？我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|want to] 睇[tai2|to watch / look / see / read] 咗[zo2|completed action] 先[sin1|first]。
   === Then when can we go to view the flat? I want to view it first.
B: 聽日[ting1jat6|tomorrow] 可以[ho2ji5|can] 去[heoi3|to go] 呀[aa3|particle]！我[ngo5|I] 幫[bong1|to help / for / on behalf of] 你[nei5|you] 約[joek3|to make an appointment / invite] 業主[jip6zyu2|landlord / property owner] 啦[laa1|particle]。
   === We can go tomorrow! I will help you make an appointment with the landlord.
```

### Dialogue 2: Discussing Rooming Arrangements

Two friends (`A` and `B`) talk about an apartment that `A` has recently rented.

```dialog
A: 我[ngo5|I] 啱啱[aam1aam1|just now / just / exactly] 租[zou1|to rent / lease] 咗[zo2|completed action] 一[jat1|one] 間[gaan1|classifier for buildings / shops / rooms] 唐樓[tong4lau2|traditional low-rise walk-up building] 嘅[ge3|possessive particle] 劏房[tong1fong2|subdivided flat / partitioned room] 呀[aa3|particle]。
   === I just rented a subdivided flat in a traditional low-rise walk-up building.
B: 吓[haa2|particle]？唐樓[tong4lau2|traditional low-rise walk-up building] 冇[mou5|not to have / without] 升降機[sing1gong3gei1|elevator / lift] 喎[wo3|particle]！你[nei5|you] 唔[m4|not] 覺得[gok3dak1|to feel / think] 辛苦[san1fu2|hard / toilsome / painstaking / exhausting] 咩[me1|particle]？
   === What? Walk-up buildings don't have elevators! Don't you feel it is exhausting?
A: 雖然[seoi1jin4|although / even though] 行[hang4|to walk / go] 樓梯[lau4tai1|stairs / staircase] 有[jau5|to have] 少少[siu2 siu2|a little bit / slightly] 辛苦[san1fu2|hard / toilsome / painstaking / exhausting]，不過[bat1gwo3|but / however] 租金[zou1gam1|rent payment / rental price] 平[peng4|cheap / inexpensive]，五[ng5|five] 千[cin1|thousand] 蚊[man1|dollar] 炸[zaa3|only (sentence-final particle)]！
   === Although walking up the stairs is a bit exhausting, the rent is cheap—it's only five thousand dollars!
B: 噉[gam2|then] 佢[keoi5|he / she] 嘅[ge3|possessive particle] 面積[min6zik1|area / size] 係[hai6|to be] 唔[m4|not] 係[hai6|to be] 好[hou2|good / fine / very / quite] 細[sai3|small / little / tiny] 呀[aa3|particle]？環境[waan4ging2|environment / surroundings] 靜[zing6|quiet / peaceful] 唔[m4|not] 靜[zing6|quiet / peaceful] 呀[aa3|particle]？
   === Then is its size very small? Is the environment quiet?
A: 實用面積[sat6jung6 min6zik1|saleable area / net floor area] 差唔多[caa1m4do1|almost / about / more or less] 一[jat1|one] 百[baak3|hundred] 五[ng5|five] 十[sap6|ten] 呎[cek3|square foot / feet] 啦[laa1|particle]。周圍[zau1wai4|around / surroundings] 好[hou2|good / fine / very / quite] 方便[fong1bin6|convenient]，不過[bat1gwo3|but / however] 日頭[jat6tau2|daytime / day] 有[jau5|to have] 少少[siu2 siu2|a little bit / slightly] 嘈[cou4|noisy]。
   === The saleable area is about one hundred and fifty square feet. The surroundings are very convenient, but it is a bit noisy during the day.
B: 唔緊要[m4 gan2jiu3|it doesn't matter / never mind / it's okay] 啦[laa1|particle]！好彩[hou2coi2|luckily / fortunately] 附近[fu6gan6|nearby / neighborhood] 方便[fong1bin6|convenient]。如果[jyu4gwo2|if / in case] 你[nei5|you] 搬屋[bun1uk1|to move house] 要[jiu3|to want / need / must / demand] 幫手[bong1sau2|to help / lend a hand]，話[waa6|to say / tell] 我[ngo5|me] 知[zi1|to know / be aware of] 啦[laa1|particle]！
   === That's alright! Luckily the neighborhood is convenient. If you need a hand moving house, let me know!
```
