In this chapter, we will learn about **Personal Savings & Investing**.

Managing daily finances, bank savings, and wealth accumulation involves standard
and highly frequent colloquial terms.

Let's dive in!

---

## 1. Savings, Investing & Account Management

Here is the essential vocabulary for managing your savings and investments in
Cantonese:

- `慳[haan1|to save (money or time)]`: To save money, reduce expenses, or
  economize.
- `投資[tau4zi1|to invest]`: To invest money, or investments.
- `定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit]`: A bank fixed-term deposit account.
- `利息[lei6sik1|interest]`: Interest earned on bank deposits or paid on loans.
- `息[sik1|interest]`: Interest or dividend.
- `基金[gei1gam1|mutual fund]`: Mutual funds.
- `長線[coeng4sin3|long term]`: A long-term horizon.
- `短線[dyun2sin3|short term]`: A short-term horizon.
- `身家[san1gaa1|net worth]`: Net worth or personal fortune.
- `最近[zeoi3gan6|recently]`: Recently.
- `噉樣[gam2joeng6|like this]`: In this way, like this.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: Colleagues Discussing Personal Finance

Two colleagues (A and B) discuss saving money and managing bank accounts during
their lunch break.

```dialog
A: 你[nei5|you] 點樣[dim2joeng6|how] 慳[haan1|to save (money or time)] 錢[cin4|money] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？我[ngo5|I] 想[soeng2|to want to] 慳[haan1|to save (money or time)] 錢[cin4|money] 做[zou6|to do] 投資[tau4zi1|to invest] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
   === How do you save money? I want to save money to make investments.
B: 我[ngo5|I] 會[wui5|will / can] 放[fong3|to release / place] 錢[cin4|money] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] 戶口[wu6hau2|account] 做[zou6|to do] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]，噉樣[gam2joeng6|like this] 可以[ho2ji5|can] 賺[zaan6|to earn] 利息[lei6sik1|interest] 嘛[maa3|sentence-final particle indicating a statement is an obvious truth or reminder]。
   === I put money in my bank account for a fixed deposit. In this way, I can earn interest.
A: 係[hai6|to be / yes] 咩[me1|sentence-final particle for doubt / what]？最近[zeoi3gan6|recently] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit] 嘅[ge3|possessive / linking particle] 息[sik1|interest] 都[dou1|also / all] 幾[gei2|quite] 好[hou2|good / very] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]！
   === Really? Recently, interest on fixed deposits is indeed quite good!
B: 係呀[hai6aa3|yes]！噉[gam2|like this / then] 你[nei5|you] 有冇[jau5mou5|do you have?] 買[maai5|buy] 基金[gei1gam1|mutual fund] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]？
   === Indeed! So do you buy mutual funds or not?
A: 我[ngo5|I] 買[maai5|buy] 過[gwo3|past experience marker / to cross / than] 基金[gei1gam1|mutual fund]，不過[bat1gwo3|but] 佢[keoi5|he / she / it] 跌[dit3|to drop] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]，我[ngo5|I] 就[zau6|then] 賣出[maai6ceot1|to sell out] 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 囉[lo1|sentence-final particle for obviousness]。
   === I have bought mutual funds before, but it fell, so I just sold it out.
B: 噉[gam2|like this / then] 你[nei5|you] 都[dou1|also / all] 係[hai6|to be / yes] 做[zou6|to do] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit] 慳[haan1|to save (money or time)] 錢[cin4|money] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！
   === Then you should also do fixed deposits to save money!
```

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of savings and investment terms in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb for "to earn / profit":
  我[ngo5|I] 慳[haan1|to save (money or time)] 錢[cin4|money] 投資[tau4zi1|to invest] ______ 咗[zo2|completed action marker] 好多[hou2do1|a lot of] 錢[cin4|money]。
answer: 賺[zaan6|to earn]
explanation: |
  `賺[zaan6|to earn]` is the colloquial Cantonese verb meaning to earn money or make a profit.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Translate the following sentence into colloquial Cantonese:
  "I put money in the bank account to do a fixed deposit to earn interest."
answer: 我[ngo5|I] 放[fong3|to release / place] 錢[cin4|money] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] 戶口[wu6hau2|account] 做[zou6|to do] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit] 賺[zaan6|to earn] 利息[lei6sik1|interest]。
explanation: |
  `我[ngo5|I]` is "I", `放[fong3|to release / place]` `錢[cin4|money]` is put money, `喺[hai2|at / in / on]` `戶口[wu6hau2|account]`, `做[zou6|to do]` `定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit]`, and `賺[zaan6|to earn]` `利息[lei6sik1|interest]`.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Which Cantonese term refers to a "long-term" investment horizon?
  A) 短線[dyun2sin3|short term]
  B) 長線[coeng4sin3|long term]
  C) 身家[san1gaa1|net worth]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `長線[coeng4sin3|long term]` literally translates to "long line," meaning a long-term investment horizon. `短線[dyun2sin3|short term]` is short-term.
```
