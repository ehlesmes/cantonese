In this chapter, we will learn about **Personal Savings & Investing**.

Managing daily finances, bank savings, and wealth accumulation involves standard
and highly frequent colloquial terms.

Let's dive in!

## 1. Savings, Investing & Account Management

Here is the essential vocabulary for managing your savings and investments in
Cantonese:

- `慳[haan1|to save (money / time) / cut down / economize]`: To save money,
  reduce expenses, or economize.
- `投資[tau4zi1|to invest / investment]`: To invest money, or investments.
- `定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit]`: A bank fixed-term deposit account.
- `利息[lei6sik1|interest (on loan/deposit)]`: Interest earned on bank deposits
  or paid on loans.
- `息[sik1|interest / dividend]`: Interest or dividend.
- `基金[gei1gam1|mutual fund]`: Mutual funds.
- `長線[coeng4sin3|long term]`: A long-term horizon.
- `短線[dyun2sin3|short term]`: A short-term horizon.
- `身家[san1gaa1|net worth / personal fortune]`: Net worth or personal fortune.
- `最近[zeoi3gan6|recently / lately]`: Recently.
- `噉樣[gam2joeng6|in this way / like this / this pattern]`: In this way, like
  this.

## 2. Situational Dialogue: Colleagues Discussing Personal Finance

Two colleagues (A and B) discuss saving money and managing bank accounts during
their lunch break.

```dialog

   === I have bought mutual funds before, but it fell, so I just sold it out.
B: 噉[gam2|then / in that case / so] 你[nei5|you] 都[dou1|also / even / already] 係[hai6|to be (am / is / are) / yes] 做[zou6|to do / make / perform] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit] 慳[haan1|to save (money / time) / cut down / economize] 錢[cin4|money] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！
   === Then you should also do fixed deposits to save money!
```

## 3. Exercises

Test your understanding of savings and investment terms in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Fill in the blank with the correct verb for "to earn / profit":
  我[ngo5|I / me] 慳[haan1|to save] 錢[cin4|money] 投資[tau4zi1|to invest] ______ 咗[zo2|completed] 好多[hou2do1|a lot of] 錢[cin4|money]。
answer: 賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money]
explanation: |
  `賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money]` is the colloquial Cantonese verb meaning to earn money or make a profit.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Translate the following sentence into colloquial Cantonese:
  "I put money in the bank account to do a fixed deposit to earn interest."
answer: 我[ngo5|I / me] 放[fong3|to put / place] 錢[cin4|money] 喺[hai2|at / in / on] 戶口[wu6hau2|account (bank/online)] 做[zou6|to do / make / perform] 定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit] 賺[zaan6|to earn / profit / make money] 利息[lei6sik1|interest (on loan/deposit)]。
explanation: |
  `我[ngo5|I / me]` is "I", `放[fong3|to put]` `錢[cin4|money]` is put money, `喺[hai2|at / in / on]` `戶口[wu6hau2|account]`, `做[zou6|to do]` `定期[ding6kei4|fixed deposit]`, and `賺[zaan6|to earn]` `利息[lei6sik1|interest]`.
```

```exercise
question: |
  Which Cantonese term refers to a "long-term" investment horizon?
  A) 短線[dyun2sin3|short term]
  B) 長線[coeng4sin3|long term]
  C) 身家[san1gaa1|net worth]
answer: B
explanation: |
  `長線[coeng4sin3|long term]` literally translates to "long line," meaning a long-term investment horizon. `短線[dyun2sin3|short term]` is short-term.
```
