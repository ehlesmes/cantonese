## 1. Apartment Layouts & Net Saleable Area

To describe your living space, Cantonese uses these terms:

- `客廳[haak3teng1|living room]`: The main living or sitting room.
- `睡房[seoi6fong2|bedroom]`: The bedroom.
- `面積[min6zik1|area]`: The total size or area of a flat.
- `實用面積[sat6jung6min6zik1|saleable area]`: The net saleable area of the
  apartment (excluding common areas and thick walls).
- `呎[cek3|square foot]`: The local standard unit of area measurement, shorthand
  for square feet.

_Example Layout Description:_

- `間[gaan1|classifier for buildings]` `屋[uk1|house]`
  `有[jau5|to have / there is]` `兩[loeng5|two]`
  `間[gaan1|classifier for buildings]` `睡房[seoi6fong2|bedroom]`
  `同[tung4|with / and]` `一[jat1|one]` `個[go3|general classifier]`
  `客廳[haak3teng1|living room]`，`實用面積[sat6jung6min6zik1|saleable area]`
  `五百[ng5baak3|five hundred]` `呎[cek3|square foot]`: The flat has two
  bedrooms and one living room, with a saleable area of five hundred square
  feet.

## 2. Neighborhood Surroundings & Cozy Living

To describe how pleasant a neighborhood is, Cantonese speakers use these terms:

- `環境[waan4ging2|environment]`: Environmental quality of a neighborhood.
- `方便[fong1bin6|convenient]`: Convenient, easy transit or shopping access.
- `周圍[zau1wai4|around]`: The surrounding area.
- `嘈[cou4|noisy]`: Noisy or loud.
- `靜[zing6|quiet]`: Quiet or peaceful.
- `好住[hou2zyu6|comfortable to live in]`: Comfortable, cozy, and pleasant to
  reside in. This uses the grammatical structure of `好[hou2|good / very]` +
  Verb (`住[zyu6|to live / stay]`) to create an adjective meaning "good to live
  in."
- `附近[fu6gan6|nearby]`: Nearby or adjacent.
- `最好[zeoi3hou2|best]`: Best or preferably.
- `搬屋[bun1uk1|to move house]`: Moving house.
- `幫手[bong1sau2|to help out]`: Helping out.

```dialog
   === The saleable area is about one hundred and fifty square feet. The surroundings are very convenient, but it is a bit noisy during the day.
B: 唔緊要[m4 gan2jiu3|it doesn't matter] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！好彩[hou2coi2|luckily] 附近[fu6gan6|nearby] 方便[fong1bin6|convenient]。如果[jyu4gwo2|if] 你[nei5|you] 搬屋[bun1uk1|to move house] 要[jiu3|to want / need] 幫手[bong1sau2|to help out]，話[waa6|to say] 我[ngo5|I] 知[zi1|to know] 啦[laa1|sentence-final particle suggesting, requesting, or encouraging action]！
   === That's alright! Luckily the neighborhood is convenient. If you need a hand moving house, let me know!
```

## 4. Exercises

Test your understanding of layout and neighborhood terms in Cantonese.

```exercise
question: |
  Translate the following sentence into colloquial Cantonese:
  "The surroundings are quiet and it is very nice to live in."
answer: 環境[waan4ging2|environment] 好[hou2|good / very] 靜[zing6|quiet]，真係[zan1hai6|really] 好住[hou2zyu6|comfortable to live in] 呀[aa3|sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly]。
explanation: |
  `環境[waan4ging2|environment]` means surroundings, `好[hou2|good / very]` is used as an intensifier, `靜[zing6|quiet]` means quiet, `真係[zan1hai6|really]` means really, and `好住[hou2zyu6|comfortable to live in]` describes comfortable residency.
```
