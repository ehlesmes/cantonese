Chapter 51

Mortgages, Loans & Getting Paid

Master colloquial Cantonese terms for salary payments, MPF, mortgages, loans, debts, and hitting targets.

In this chapter, we will learn about Mortgages, Loans & Getting Paid.

Discussing salaries, paying bills, and managing loans is an essential part of daily life in Hong Kong. We will learn standard and highly frequent colloquial terms for these situations.

Let's dive in!

1. Mortgages, Loans & Targets

Here is the essential vocabulary for managing bills, loans, and salary in Cantonese:

  • 出糧ceot1loeng4
    getting paid / salary    : Getting paid your salary (literally "producing grain").
  • 強積金koeng5zik1gam1
    MPF / mandatory provident fund    : Mandatory Provident Fund retirement scheme.
  • 供樓gung1lau2
    to pay a mortgage    : To pay a mortgage or make housing installments.
    • Example: 佢keoi5
      he / she / it       每mui5
      every       個go3
      general measure word / individual / unit       月jyut6
      month       要jiu3
      need       俾bei2
      to give / for / let / allow       兩loeng5
      two       萬maan6
      ten thousand       蚊man1
      dollar       供樓gung1lau2
      to pay a mortgage      : He needs to pay twenty thousand dollars every month for the mortgage.
  • 借ze3
    to borrow / lend    : To borrow or lend. The direction depends on the structure:
    • 問man6
      to ask       人jan4
      person / people       借錢ze3cin4
      to borrow/lend money      : To borrow money from someone.
    • 借錢ze3cin4
      to borrow/lend money       俾bei2
      to give / for / let / allow       人jan4
      person / people      : To lend money to someone.
  • 還waan4
    to return / repay    : To return borrowed money or repay a loan.
  • 跑數paau2sou3
    to hit a sales target / chase sales numbers    : To chase sales targets or hit a financial quota (literally "running numbers").

A) 強積金koeng5zik1gam1
MPF

C) 供樓gung1lau2
paying mortgage

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: B

出糧ceot1loeng4
getting paid one's salary literally translates to "producing grain" and is the colloquial Cantonese term for getting paid one's salary.
