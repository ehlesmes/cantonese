Chapter 34

Clothing & Shopping Classifiers

Master essential classifiers in Cantonese for clothing, shoes, hats, and other shopping items.

In this chapter, we will master the correct classifiers (measure words) to use when shopping for clothes, accessories, and shoes in Cantonese.

1. Essential Clothing & Shopping Classifiers

Using the correct classifier (measure word) is essential to sounding natural when referring to different items in a shop or market. Avoid using 個go3
general measure word for everything! Here are the core classifiers for shopping:

  • 件gin6
    measure word for clothes, matters, and items    : Used for upper-body clothing (shirts, jackets, coats).
    • Example: 呢ni1
      this       件gin6
      measure word for clothes, matters, and items       衫saam1
      clothes / clothing / shirt       (this shirt)
  • 條tiu4
    measure word for long, thin objects    : Used for long, thin items, including pants, skirts, ties, and necklaces.
    • Example: 呢ni1
      this       條tiu4
      measure word for long, thin objects       褲fu3
      pants / trousers       (these pants)
  • 頂ding2
    measure word for hats    : Exclusively used for hats and caps.
    • Example: 呢ni1
      this       頂ding2
      measure word for hats       帽mou2
      hat / cap       (this hat)
  • 對deoi3
    pair / couple    : Used for paired items like shoes, socks, or earrings.
    • Example: 呢ni1
      this       對deoi3
      pair / couple       鞋haai4
      shoes       (these shoes)
  • 隻zek3
    measure word for animals or objects in pairs    : Used to refer to just one item of a pair.
    • Example: 呢ni1
      this       隻zek3
      measure word for animals or objects in pairs       鞋haai4
      shoes       (this single shoe)

3. Exercises

Use these exercises to test your understanding of Cantonese classifiers.

Practice Exercise

Which classifier should you use to ask for the price of a pair of shoes?
A) 件gin6
measure word for clothes, matters, and items
B) 條tiu4
measure word for long, thin objects
C) 對deoi3
pair / couple
D) 頂ding2
measure word for hats

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: C

對deoi3
pair / couple is the standard classifier for paired items like shoes or socks.

Practice Exercise

Which classifier is used for upper-body clothing like shirts and jackets?
A) 條tiu4
measure word for long, thin objects
B) 頂ding2
measure word for hats
C) 件gin6
measure word for clothes, matters, and items

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: C

件gin6
measure word for clothes, matters, and items is the correct classifier for shirts, jackets, and coats.
