Chapter 18

Past Experience & Education

Learn how to describe life experiences using the aspect particle gwo, and master education and schooling vocabulary in Cantonese.

In this chapter, we will study how to talk about life experiences using the experiential aspect particle gwo, and learn essential terminology for education and schooling.

Let's dive in!

1. Talking About Past Experience: Aspect Particle 過gwo3
verbal aspect suffix indicating past experience / 'have done before'

To express that you have done something before at least once in your life (the experiential aspect), Cantonese uses the particle 過gwo3
verbal aspect suffix indicating past experience / 'have done before'.

The Experiential Structure

The particle 過gwo3
verbal aspect suffix indicating past experience / 'have done before' is placed directly after the main verb:

Formula: [Subject] + [Verb] + 過gwo3
verbal aspect suffix indicating past experience / 'have done before' + [Object]

Let's look at some examples:

我ngo5
I / me 讀duk6
to study / read (aloud) 過gwo3
verbal aspect suffix indicating past experience / 'have done before' 大學daai6hok6
university 呀aa3
sentence-final particle softening statements, making questions friendly。
I have studied at university before.
我ngo5
I / me 走zau2
to run / leave / escape 過gwo3
verbal aspect suffix indicating past experience / 'have done before' 堂tong4
class / hall 囉lo1
sentence-final particle showing obviousness, resignation, or 'of course'！
I have skipped class before, obviously!

Negating Experiences: 冇mou5
to not have / there is not

To say that you have never done something, place 冇mou5
to not have / there is not before the verb, but keep the 過gwo3
verbal aspect suffix indicating past experience / 'have done before' after the verb!

Formula: [Subject] + 冇mou5
to not have / there is not + [Verb] + 過gwo3
verbal aspect suffix indicating past experience / 'have done before' + [Object]
