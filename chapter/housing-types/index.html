Chapter 38

Types of Housing & Apartments

Master spoken Cantonese terms for different types of residential buildings and apartment styles in Hong Kong.

Hong Kong’s high-density urban environment has led to unique residential structures. When navigating the housing market, it is essential to distinguish between different types of buildings.

Let's dive in!

1. Types of Housing in Hong Kong

When looking for a place to live, you will encounter various housing options:

  • 唐樓tong4lau2
    traditional low-rise walk-up building    : These are older low-rise buildings (typically 5 to 8 stories) constructed without a 升降機sing1gong3gei1
    elevator / lift    . They are famous for their high ceilings and historical value.
  • 洋樓joeng4lau2
    modern high-rise building with elevator    : These are modern mid-to-high-rise residential buildings equipped with a 升降機sing1gong3gei1
    elevator / lift     and usually have a security guard at the lobby.
  • 套房tou3fong2
    studio flat / ensuite bedroom    : A studio apartment or a single self-contained bedroom containing its own private bathroom.
  • 劏房tong1fong2
    subdivided flat / partitioned room    : A flat that has been partitioned or split into multiple tiny individual units.
  • 公屋gung1uk1
    public housing    : Public housing estates.
  • 屋uk1
    house / room    : A general term for a house or room.
  • 樓lau2
    building / flat / apartment / floor    : A generic term that can mean a building, a flat, an apartment, or a floor.

3. Exercises

Practice Exercise

Which of the following describes a traditional low-rise walk-up building that does not have an elevator?
A) 洋樓joeng4lau2
modern high-rise building with elevator
B) 唐樓tong4lau2
traditional low-rise walk-up building
C) 劏房tong1fong2
subdivided flat / partitioned room
D) 套房tou3fong2
studio flat / ensuite bedroom

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: B

唐樓tong4lau2
traditional low-rise walk-up building refers specifically to older, low-rise walk-up buildings in Hong Kong without elevator access.
