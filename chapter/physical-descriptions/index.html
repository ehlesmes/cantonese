Chapter 19

Physical Appearance & Noun Modification

Master vocabulary for physical appearance and clothing, and learn how to link descriptions using the particle ge3.

In this chapter, we will learn vocabulary to describe physical appearance and clothing, and master the critical noun modification particle ge3 in Cantonese.

Let's dive in!

1. Vocabulary: Physical Appearance & Clothing

Let's learn some useful vocabulary for describing people and what they wear:

  • 靚仔leng3zai2
    handsome boy / handsome     — handsome
  • 靚女leng3neoi5
    pretty girl / beautiful     — pretty / beautiful

2. Noun Modification & Complex Descriptions: 嘅ge3
possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle

The particle 嘅ge3
possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle is one of the most frequently used words in Cantonese. We have already seen it as a possessive marker (similar to English 's). Now, we will see how it works as a general noun modifier and a relative clause linker.

1. Linking Adjectives to Nouns

When you want to describe a noun with a multi-syllable adjective or emphasize an attribute, you link them using 嘅ge3
possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle:

  • 靚leng3
    pretty / beautiful / handsome / high quality     嘅ge3
    possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle     衫saam1
    clothes / clothing / shirt     — pretty clothes / pretty shirt
  • 貴gwai3
    expensive     嘅ge3
    possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle     鞋haai4
    shoes     — expensive shoes
  • 平peng4
    cheap / inexpensive     嘅ge3
    possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle     電腦din6nou5
    computer     — cheap computer

Note: For simple, single-character adjectives, native speakers often omit 嘅ge3
possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle in fast speech if it forms a common compound (e.g., linking 大daai6
big / large / great and 衫saam1
clothes / clothing / shirt directly as a compound). If you use 嘅ge3
possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle with a single-character adjective, you usually need an intensifier like 好hou2
good / fine / very / quite or a measure word. For example, 大daai6
big 嘅ge3
modifier 衫saam1
shirt sounds unnatural, whereas 好hou2
very 大daai6
big 嘅ge3
modifier 衫saam1
shirt or just 大daai6
big 衫saam1
shirt sounds natural.

2. Linking Phrases & Relative Clauses

In English, relative clauses ("the clothes that I bought", "the language that you speak") are placed after the noun they modify, using helper words like "that", "which", or "who".

In Cantonese, the modifying clause is placed before the noun and linked by 嘅ge3
possessive particle / nominalizing particle / final assertive particle:
