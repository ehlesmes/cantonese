Chapter 48

Internet & Device Basics

Master colloquial Cantonese for surfing the web, downloading apps, connecting to Wi-Fi, and handling device freezes.

In this chapter, we will learn about Internet & Device Basics.

We will explore basic online networking terms, how to navigate device freezes, and study everyday tech vocabulary.

Let's dive in!

1. Internet & Device Basics

To describe daily web browsing and computer situations, spoken Cantonese uses:

  • 上網soeng5mong5
    to go online / surf the internet    : To go online or surf the web.
  • 網上mong5soeng6
    online    : Adjective/adverb describing online operations or sites.
  • 下載haa6zoi3
    to download    : To download files or software.
  • 智能電話zi3nang4 din6waa2
    smartphone    : A smartphone.
  • 智能zi3nang4
    smart / intelligent (tech)    : Smart.
  • WiFiwai1faai1
    Wi-Fi / wireless internet    : Wi-Fi.
  • 網絡mong5lok3
    internet / network    : Internet connection or network.
  • 網mong5
    internet / net / network    : Internet.
  • 死機sei2gei1
    device freeze / crash    : Device crash or freeze (literally "dead machine").

Practice Exercise

Which Cantonese phrase literally meaning "dead machine" is used when a device freezes or crashes?
A) 爆數據baau3 sou3geoi3
exceed data limit
B) 聲控sing1hung3
voice control
C) 死機sei2gei1
device crash
D) 開關hoi1gwaan1
switch

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: C

死機sei2gei1
device crash (literally "dead machine") is the standard colloquial term for a frozen or crashed device.
