Chapter 40

Neighborhood Slang & Local Life

Master apartment layout descriptions, square footage measurements, and neighborhood surroundings vocabulary in Cantonese.

In this chapter, we will learn about Neighborhood Slang & Local Life.

To describe your living space and neighborhood surroundings in detail, you need to master specialized layout measurements and descriptive adjectives.

Let's dive in!

1. Apartment Layouts & Net Saleable Area

To describe your living space, Cantonese uses these terms:

  • 客廳haak3teng1
    living room / parlor    : The main living or sitting room.
  • 睡房seoi6fong2
    bedroom    : The bedroom.
  • 面積min6zik1
    area / size    : The total size or area of a flat.
  • 實用面積sat6jung6min6zik1
    saleable area / net floor area    : The net saleable area of the apartment (excluding common areas and thick walls).
  • 呎cek3
    square foot / feet    : The local standard unit of area measurement, shorthand for square feet.

    five hundred     呎cek3
    square feet    : The flat has two bedrooms and one living room, with a saleable area of five hundred square feet.

2. Neighborhood Surroundings & Cozy Living

To describe how pleasant a neighborhood is, Cantonese speakers use these terms:

  • 環境waan4ging2
    environment / surroundings    : Environmental quality of a neighborhood.
  • 方便fong1bin6
    convenient    : Convenient, easy transit or shopping access.
  • 周圍zau1wai4
    around / surroundings    : The surrounding area.
  • 嘈cou4
    noisy    : Noisy or loud.
  • 靜zing6
    quiet / peaceful    : Quiet or peaceful.
  • 好住hou2zyu6
    nice to live in / comfortable to reside    : Comfortable, cozy, and pleasant to reside in. This uses the grammatical structure of 好hou2
    good     + Verb (住zyu6
    to live    ) to create an adjective meaning "good to live in."
  • 附近fu6gan6
    nearby / neighborhood    : Nearby or adjacent.
  • 最好zeoi3hou2
    best / preferably    : Best or preferably.
  • 搬屋bun1uk1
    to move house    : Moving house.
  • 幫手bong1sau2
    to help / lend a hand    : Helping out.
