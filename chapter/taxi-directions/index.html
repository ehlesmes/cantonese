Chapter 12

Directional Commands & Taxi Navigation

Master spoken directional commands like straight, turn left, and turn right to instruct drivers in Cantonese.

In this chapter, we will learn how to give basic directional commands in Cantonese, perfect for instructing a taxi or ride-share driver.

Let's dive in!

1. Directional Commands

Here are the basic commands for navigating streets in Cantonese:

  • 直行zik6haang4
    to go straight    : Go straight ahead.
  • 轉左zyun2zo2
    to turn left    : Turn left.
  • 轉右zyun2jau6
    to turn right    : Turn right.
  • 過馬路gwo3maa5lou6
    to cross the road    : Cross the street.

To link these directions together sequentially, use 然後jin4hau6
then / afterwards:

  • 直行zik6haang4
    go straight    ，然後jin4hau6
    then     轉左zyun2zo2
    turn left    : Go straight, then turn left.

3. Exercises

Review the directional commands introduced in this chapter:

Practice Exercise

Fill in the blank to say "Go straight, then turn left":
直行zik6haang4
go straight，然後jin4hau6
then ____。

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: 轉左zyun2zo2
turn left

直行zik6haang4
to go straight means go straight, 然後jin4hau6
then / afterwards means then, and 轉左zyun2zo2
to turn left means turn left.

Practice Exercise

Which of the following is the correct Cantonese phrase for "to cross the road"?
A) 轉左zyun2zo2
turn left
B) 直行zik6haang4
go straight
C) 過馬路gwo3maa5lou6
cross the road
D) 轉右zyun2jau6
turn right

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: C

過馬路gwo3maa5lou6
to cross the road is the standard Cantonese verb phrase for crossing the street.
