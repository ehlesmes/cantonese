Chapter 24

Modern Tech, Smart Living & App Slang

Spoken terms for internet, mobile apps, smart home, and app slang.

1. Internet & Smartphone Basics

In modern Hong Kong, technology is deeply integrated into daily life. Talking about basic digital activities in colloquial Cantonese uses a blend of native characters and highly integrated English loanwords:

  • 上網soeng5mong5
    to go online / surf the internet    : To go online, surf the internet, or connect to the web.
  • 網上mong5soeng6
    online    : Used as an adjective or adverb to describe online activities, games, or services.
  • 下載haa6zoi3
    to download    : To download files, videos, or applications.
  • 智能電話zi3nang4 din6waa2
    smartphone    : A smartphone.
  • 智能zi3nang4
    smart / intelligent (tech)    : Smart or intelligent when referring to modern technology.
  • WiFiwai1faai1
    Wi-Fi / wireless internet    : Wi-Fi or wireless internet connection.
  • 網絡mong5lok6
    internet / network    : Internet connection or digital network.
  • 網mong5
    internet / net / network    : Short form for the internet or a network.

2. Apps, Messaging & Social Media Slang

Hong Kong social media and app culture has created incredibly colorful spoken vocabulary. Many terms are direct Cantonese phonetics of English loanwords or creative internet slang:

  • Appep1
    mobile application / app    : Pronounced like the English "app" but with a classic Cantonese short, clipped tone ep1.
  • 些牙se1aa4
    to share (loanword)    : A highly popular loanword from English "share," meaning to share a link, photo, or post.
  • IGai1zi1
    Instagram (slang)    : Highly integrated colloquial term for Instagram.
  • FBef1bi1
    Facebook (slang)    : Colloquial term for Facebook.
  • WhatsAppep1si1ep1
    WhatsApp / to WhatsApp someone    : Refers to the WhatsApp messaging app, and is extremely common as a verb meaning "to WhatsApp someone."
  • 傳cyun4
    to send / transmit / pass on    : To send or transmit messages, photos, or files.
  • 發送faat3sung3
    to send / transmit    : To send or transmit.
  • 覆fuk1
    to reply / write back    : The standard spoken verb to reply to a text, email, or message.
  • 留言lau4jin4
    to leave a message / comment    : To leave a comment or message on a post.
  • 短訊dyun2seon3
    text message / SMS    : A text message or SMS.
  • 錄音luk6jam1
    voice recording / to record audio    : A voice note/message, or the act of recording audio.
  • 片pin2
    video / movie / short clip    : A video or short clip.

3. Smart Living & Tech Crises Slang

As smart home technology becomes more popular, terms like voice control are common. Additionally, managing device batteries and data plans has birthed standard spoken slangs:

  • 智能家居zi3nang4 gaa1geoi1
    smart home    : A smart home system.
  • 智能鎖zi3nang4 so2
    smart lock    : A smart lock.
  • 開關hoi1gwaan1
    switch / to turn on or off    : A switch, or to turn a device on or off.
  • 聲控sing1hung3
    voice-controlled / voice control    : Voice-controlled or using voice command.
  • 熄sik1
    to turn off / extinguish (a light, device, etc.)    : To turn off a device or extinguish a light.
  • 燈dang1
    light / lamp    : A light or lamp.
  • 鎖so2
    to lock / lock    : To lock something, or a lock itself.
  • 火牛fo2ngau4
    power adapter / charger (slang)    : Literally "fire cow," the extremely popular local slang for a power adapter or wall charger.
  • 尿袋niu6doi2
    portable power bank (slang)    : Literally "urine bag," the classic, universal Hong Kong slang for a portable power bank.
  • 充電cung1din6
    to charge a battery    : To charge a battery.
  • 數據sou3geoi3
    data    : Mobile data or cellular data.
  • 爆數據baau3 sou3geoi3
    to exceed mobile data limit (slang)    : Literally "explode data," meaning to exceed your monthly mobile data plan limit.
  • 密碼mat6maa5
    password    : Password.
  • 戶口wu6hau2
    account (bank/online)    : A user account.

A) 火牛fo2ngau4

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: B

尿袋niu6doi2

power adapter / charger (slang) is the slang for a wall adapter/charger.
