Chapter 25

Apartment Rentals & Layouts

Master spoken Cantonese terms for flat-hunting, residential buildings, and rental payments.

In this chapter, we will learn about Apartment Rentals & Layouts.

Hong Kong’s high-density urban environment has led to unique residential structures. When navigating the housing market, it is essential to distinguish between different types of buildings and understand the rental process.

Let's dive in!

1. Types of Housing in Hong Kong

When flat-hunting, you will encounter various housing options:

  • 唐樓tong4lau2
    traditional low-rise walk-up building    : These are older low-rise buildings (typically 5 to 8 stories) constructed without a 升降機sing1gong3gei1
    elevator / lift    . They are famous for their high ceilings and historical value.
  • 洋樓joeng4lau2
    modern high-rise building with elevator    : These are modern mid-to-high-rise residential buildings equipped with a 升降機sing1gong3gei1
    elevator / lift     and usually have a security guard at the lobby.
  • 套房tou3fong2
    studio flat / ensuite bedroom    : A studio apartment or a single self-contained bedroom containing its own private bathroom.
  • 劏房tong1fong2
    subdivided flat / partitioned room    : A flat that has been partitioned or split into multiple tiny individual units.
  • 公屋gung1uk1
    public housing    : Public housing estates.
  • 屋uk1
    house / room    : A general term for a house or room.
  • 樓lau2
    building / flat / apartment / floor    : A generic term that can mean a building, a flat, an apartment, or a floor.

2. Flat-Hunting & Rental Terminology

The process of finding and renting a flat involves specific vocabulary:

  • 搵樓wan2lau2
    to look for apartments / flat-hunting    : The action of searching for an apartment.
  • 睇樓tai2lau2
    to view apartments / flats    : Going to view apartments in person.
  • 租zou1
    to rent / lease    : To rent or lease a property.
  • 租客zou1haak3
    tenant / renter    : The tenant.
  • 業主jip6zyu2
    landlord / property owner    : The landlord or property owner.
  • 租金zou1gam1
    rent payment / rental price    : The monthly rent.
  • 按金on3gam1
    deposit    : Security deposit.
  • 上期soeng5kei4
    rent paid in advance (first month rent)    : First month of rent paid in advance.
  • 兩按一上loeng5on3jat1soeng5
    two months deposit and one month rent in advance    : The standard Hong Kong upfront payment scheme.
  • 合約hap6joek3
    contract / agreement    : The formal lease contract.
  • 佣金jung2gam1
    commission fee    : Agency commission fee.
  • 屋苑uk1jyun2
    housing estate / complex    : A housing estate or complex.
  • 管理費gun2lei5fai3
    management fee    : Building management fee.

A) 洋樓joeng4lau2

B) 唐樓tong4lau2
traditional low-rise walk-up building

subdivided flat / partitioned room

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: B

唐樓tong4lau2
