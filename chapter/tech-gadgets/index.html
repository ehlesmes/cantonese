Chapter 32

Computers, Hardware & Smart Home Tech

Master spoken Cantonese terms for computer components, Wi-Fi networks, smart home voice controls, and device power chargers.

In this chapter, we will learn about Computers, Hardware & Smart Home Tech.

We will explore basic online networking terms, how to navigate device freezes, and study local slangs for chargers and power adapters, alongside smart home voice command structures.

Let's dive in!

1. Internet & Device Basics

To describe daily web browsing and computer situations, spoken Cantonese uses:

  • 上網soeng5mong5
    to go online / surf the internet    : To go online or surf the web.
  • 網上mong5soeng6
    online    : Adjective/adverb describing online operations or sites.
  • 下載haa6zoi3
    to download    : To download files or software.
  • 智能電話zi3nang4 din6waa2
    smartphone    : A smartphone.
  • 智能zi3nang4
    smart / intelligent (tech)    : Smart.
  • WiFiwai1faai1
    Wi-Fi / wireless internet    : Wi-Fi.
  • 網絡mong5lok6
    internet / network    : Internet connection or network.
  • 網mong5
    internet / net / network    : Internet.
  • 死機sei2gei1
    device freeze / crash    : Device crash or freeze (literally "dead machine").

2. Smart Home & Tech Crises Slang

Managing your home systems, electrical sockets, and device charges involves unique terminology:

  • 智能家居zi3nang4 gaa1geoi1
    smart home    : Smart home systems.
  • 智能鎖zi3nang4 so2
    smart lock    : A smart lock.
  • 冷氣laang5hei3
    air conditioning / AC    : Air conditioning or AC.
  • 開關hoi1gwaan1
    switch / to turn on or off    : A switch, or to turn on/off.
  • 聲控sing1hung3
    voice-controlled / voice control    : Voice-controlled.
  • 熄sik1
    to turn off / extinguish (a light, device, etc.)    : To turn off a device/light.
  • 燈dang1
    light / lamp    : A light or lamp.
  • 鎖so2
    to lock / lock    : To lock, or a lock itself.
  • 火牛fo2ngau4
    power adapter / charger (slang)    : Literally "fire cow," the popular slang for a power adapter/wall charger.
  • 尿袋niu6doi2
    portable power bank (slang)    : Literally "urine bag," the slang for a portable power bank.
  • 充電cung1din6
    to charge a battery    : To charge a battery.
  • 數據sou3geoi3
    data    : Mobile data.
  • 爆數據baau3 sou3geoi3
    to exceed mobile data limit (slang)    : Exceeding mobile data plans.
  • 密碼mat6maa5
    password    : Password.
  • 戶口wu6hau2
    account (bank/online)    : User account.
  • 就快zau6faai3
    soon / about to    : Soon, about to.
  • 百分之十baak3fan6zi1sap6
    ten percent    : Ten percent.
  • 插頭caap3tau4
    electrical socket / plug    : Electrical socket or plug.
  • 牆coeng4
    wall    : Wall.
  • 系統hai6tung2
    system    : System.
  • 大聲daai6sing1
    loudly / loud voice    : Loudly.

4. Exercises

Test your understanding of computers, smart home devices, and power bank slangs.

Practice Exercise

Which of the following refers to a portable power bank in Hong Kong slang?
A) 火牛fo2ngau4
power adapter
B) 智能鎖zi3nang4 so2
smart lock
C) 尿袋niu6doi2
power bank
D) 系統hai6tung2
system

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: C

尿袋niu6doi2
power bank (literally "urine bag") is the ubiquitous Hong Kong slang for a portable power bank. 火牛fo2ngau4
power adapter refers to a power wall adapter.
