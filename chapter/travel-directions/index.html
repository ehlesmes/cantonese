Chapter 6

Directions & Spatial Navigation

Master colloquial directions, street landmarks, and the directional complement dou in Cantonese.

In this chapter, we will learn how to ask for and give Directions & Spatial Navigation.

When exploring the city or directing a driver, spoken Cantonese relies on simple directional commands and road features. Let's study how to describe movement.

Let's dive in!

1. Directional Commands

Here are the basic commands for navigating streets in Cantonese:

  • 直行zik6haang4
    to go straight    : Go straight ahead.
  • 轉左zyun2zo2
    to turn left    : Turn left.
  • 轉右zyun2jau6
    to turn right    : Turn right.
  • 過馬路gwo3maa5lou6
    to cross the road    : Cross the street.

To link these directions together sequentially, use 然後jin4hau6
then / afterwards:

  • 直行zik6haang4
    go straight    ，然後jin4hau6
    then     轉左zyun2zo2
    turn left    : Go straight, then turn left.

2. Street Landmarks & Infrastructure

  • 前邊cin4bin1
    ahead / in front / up ahead    : Ahead or in front.
  • 路口lou6hau2
    intersection / junction    : An intersection or street corner.
  • 燈位dang1wai2
    traffic light / intersection with lights    : Traffic lights.
  • 天橋tin1kiu4
    pedestrian flyover / footbridge    : A pedestrian bridge over the street.
  • 隧道seoi6dou6
    tunnel    : An underpass or tunnel.

Time & Delay States

If you are rushing or walking somewhere:

  • 行路haang4lou6
    to walk    : Traveling on foot.
  • 趕時間gon2si4gaan3
    in a hurry / rushing for time    : Being in a hurry.
  • 遲到ci4dou3
    to arrive late    : Arriving late.
  • 等dang2
    to wait    : Waiting.
  • 幾耐gei2noi6
    how long (time)    : Asking about the duration of time.

3. The Directional Complement 到dou3
to arrive / until

The particle 到dou3
to arrive / until is frequently used as a verbal complement to indicate the destination or endpoint of an action. When paired with movement verbs, it links the action directly to the landmark:

  • 行到haang4dou3
    walk until / walk to     前邊cin4bin1
    ahead     路口lou6hau2
    intersection    : Walk until the intersection ahead.

5. Exercises

Test your understanding of directional commands and landmarks in Cantonese.

Practice Exercise

Fill in the blank to say "Go straight, then turn left":
直行zik6haang4
go straight，然後jin4hau6
then ____。

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: 轉左zyun2zo2
turn left

直行zik6haang4
to go straight means go straight, 然後jin4hau6
then / afterwards means then, and 轉左zyun2zo2
to turn left means turn left.

Practice Exercise

What is the function of the complement 到dou3
to arrive / until in "行到haang4dou3
walk to 前邊cin4bin1
ahead / in front / up ahead 路口lou6hau2
intersection / junction"?
A) It indicates continuous action in progress.
B) It shows that the action has been completed.
C) It links the action of walking to its destination/endpoint.
D) It forms a polite request.

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: C

The complement 到dou3
to arrive / until is used with movement verbs to indicate walking until or arriving at a destination.
