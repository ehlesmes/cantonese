Chapter 33

Market Bargaining & Spoken Prices

Master currency terms, spoken price structures, and the art of bargaining in Cantonese street markets.

In this chapter, we will learn how to handle spoken prices, currency structures, and the art of bargaining in Cantonese.

1. Currency & Spoken Prices in Cantonese

In daily Cantonese life, handling money and pricing is highly colloquial and differs significantly from formal written Chinese. When asking for prices, instead of the formal 幾多gei2do1
how much 錢cin2
money, you will frequently hear speakers use the shorter, snappier 幾錢gei2 cin4
how much money in casual street environments.

The Units of Currency

To talk about currency, spoken Cantonese employs unique units:

  • 蚊man1
    dollar    : The standard spoken unit of currency (equivalent to the written Hong Kong dollar symbol).
  • 毫hou4
    ten cents / dime    : Used for ten-cent intervals.
  • 毫子hou4 zi2
    ten-cent coin / dime    : The physical coin of ten cents.
  • 分fan1
    cent / minute    : Used for single cents (though rarely seen in modern transactions).

2. The Art of Bargaining (講價gong2 gaa3
to bargain)

If you are shopping in Hong Kong’s vibrant street markets (like the Ladies' Market in Mong Kok or Temple Street in Yau Ma Tei), bargaining (講價gong2 gaa3
to bargain) is part of the fun and local culture.

Here are the key verbs and expressions you need to bargain effectively with the 老闆lou5baan2
boss / proprietor:

  • 平peng4
    cheap     啲di1
    a bit     啦laa1
    particle    : The ultimate gold-standard bargaining phrase. It uses 平peng4
    cheap     with the comparative suffix 啲di1
    a bit     and the softening particle 啦laa1
    particle    .
  • 減價gaam2 gaa3
    to reduce price / go on sale    : To ask the boss to mark down the price.
  • 特價dat6 gaa3
    special price / bargain price    : Refer to items on a special bargain sale.
  • 有折jau5 zit3
    to have a discount     / 打折daa2 zit3
    to give a discount    : Referring to discounts. E.g., asking if there is a discount (折zit3
    discount    ).
  • 送sung3
    to give as a gift / include for free    : Ask for free additions or gifts (e.g., buy this and get that for free).
  • 算啦syun3 laa1
    forget it / let it be    : The ultimate walk-away strategy! If they won't budge on price, say 算啦syun3 laa1
    forget it / let it be     and walk. They will often call you back with a lower price!
