Chapter 7

Local Transport Modes & Minibus Slang

Learn how to ride Hong Kong's public transit system, pay using Octopus cards, and shout minibus stopping requests like a local.

In this chapter, we will learn how to navigate Local Transport Modes & Minibus Slang.

Getting around Hong Kong requires understanding the core vocabulary of daily transit. The standard spoken verb for taking any transit mode is 搭車daap3ce1
to take transit / ride a vehicle. If you need to transfer between lines or change vehicles, the term is 轉車zyun2ce1
to transfer (vehicles).

Let's dive in!

1. Public Transit Modes

Cantonese speakers use a mixture of traditional characters and colloquial English loanwords to describe transport options:

  • 地鐵dei6tit3
    MTR / subway    : The mass transit railway system in Hong Kong.
  • 的士dik1si2
    taxi    : The phonetic loanword for taxi.
  • 巴士baa1si2
    bus    : The phonetic loanword for bus.
  • 紅hung4
    red     VANman1
    minibus (loanword)    : The iconic red minibuses, which have flexible routes and schedules.
  • 綠luk6
    green     VANman1
    minibus (loanword)    : The green minibuses, which run on fixed schedules and routes.
  • 船syun4
    ferry / boat     / 天星小輪tin1sing1siu2leon4
    Star Ferry    : Ferries, including the iconic Star Ferry.
  • 電車din6ce1
    tram     / 叮叮ding1ding1
    tram (colloquial)    : The historic double-decker trams on Hong Kong Island.

Transit Locations & Payments

To locate stops or pay for your fare, use these terms:

  • 站zaam6
    station / stop    : The generic term for station or stop.
  • 地鐵站dei6tit3zaam6
    MTR station / subway station    : MTR subway station.
  • 巴士站baa1si2zaam6
    bus stop    : Bus stop.
  • 的士站dik1si2zaam6
    taxi stand    : Taxi stand.
  • 八達通baat3daat6tung1
    Octopus card    : The ubiquitous local contactless smart card. To use it, you 嘟dut1
    to tap a card / scan / beep    .

2. Minibus Shouting Etiquette

Riding a minibus (紅hung4
red VANman1
minibus (loanword) or 綠luk6
green VANman1
minibus (loanword)) is a quintessential Hong Kong experience. Unlike double-decker buses, minibuses do not have stop buttons or automated announcements. Instead, passengers must shout out to the 司機si1gei1
driver when they want to request a stop.

The Stop Request Template

To request a stop, use this formula:

  • 唔該m4goi1
    please     + Landmark/Location + 有落jau5lok6
    stop here please

Common Stop Request Expressions

Here are the most common phrases passengers shout on a minibus:

  • 唔該m4goi1
    please     前邊cin4bin1
    ahead     有落jau5lok6
    stop here please    : Stop up ahead, please.
  • 唔該m4goi1
    please     路口lou6hau2
    intersection     有落jau5lok6
    stop here please    : Stop at the intersection, please.
  • 唔該m4goi1
    please     燈位dang1wai2
    traffic light     有落jau5lok6
    stop here please    : Stop at the traffic light, please.
  • 唔該m4goi1
    please     地鐵站dei6tit3zaam6
    MTR station / subway station     有落jau5lok6
    stop here please    : Stop at the MTR station, please.

To get on a vehicle, use 上車soeng5ce1
to get on a vehicle, and to get off, use 落車lok6ce1
to get off a vehicle.
