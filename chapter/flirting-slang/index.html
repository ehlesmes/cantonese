Chapter 36

Flirting & soldiering Slang

Explore Cantonese slangs for secret crushes, ideal partners, and the unique local metaphor of 'soldiering'.

In this chapter, we will learn about the dynamics of flirting and the unique colloquial terms for crushes and unrequited admiration in Hong Kong.

1. The Dynamics of Flirting & "Soldiering"

Cantonese pop culture and student dynamics have created distinct humorous terms for crushes and unrequited admiration.

Crushes & Ideals

  • 暗戀ngam3lyun2
    to crush on / love secretly    : To have a secret crush on someone.
  • 女神neoi5san4
    goddess / ideal woman    : An ideal woman or female crush.
  • 男神naam4san4
    male god / ideal man    : An ideal man or male crush.

The Concept of "Soldiering" (收兵sau1bing1
to collect platonic admirers)

One of the most unique local slang metaphors is:

  • 收兵sau1bing1
    to collect platonic admirers (literally 'collect soldiers')    : The act of keeping platonic admirers around to do favors, buy gifts, or drive them around, without ever intending to date them.
  • 兵bing1
    platonic admirer who is led on (literally 'soldier')    : A platonic admirer who is led on and does favors for their crush (the "goddess") in the hope of eventually dating them.
