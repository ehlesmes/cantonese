Chapter 50

Banking & Savings

Master colloquial Cantonese terms for saving money, fixed deposits, mutual funds, and long-term investments.

In this chapter, we will learn about Personal Savings & Investing.

Managing daily finances, bank savings, and wealth accumulation involves standard and highly frequent colloquial terms.

Let's dive in!

1. Savings, Investing & Account Management

Here is the essential vocabulary for managing your savings and investments in Cantonese:

  • 慳haan1
    to save (money/time) / cut down / economize    : To save money, reduce expenses, or economize.
  • 投資tau4zi1
    to invest / investment    : To invest money, or investments.
  • 定期ding6kei4
    fixed deposit / term deposit    : A bank fixed-term deposit account.
  • 利息lei6sik1
    interest (on loan/deposit)    : Interest earned on bank deposits or paid on loans.
  • 息sik1
    interest / dividend    : Interest or dividend.
  • 基金gei1gam1
    fund / mutual fund    : Mutual funds.
  • 長線coeng4sin3
    long term    : A long-term horizon.
  • 短線dyun2sin3
    short term    : A short-term horizon.
  • 身家san1gaa1
    net worth / personal fortune    : Net worth or personal fortune.
  • 最近zeoi3gan6
    recently / lately    : Recently.
  • 噉樣gam2joeng6
    in this way / like this / this pattern    : In this way, like this.
