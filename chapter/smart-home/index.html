Chapter 49

Smart Home & Tech Crises

Master colloquial Cantonese terms for smart home voice commands, chargers, power banks, and handling mobile data limits.

In this chapter, we will learn about Smart Home & Tech Crises.

We will explore voice commands for home appliances, learn local slang terms for chargers, and study other everyday smart home vocabulary.

Let's dive in!

1. Smart Home & Tech Crises Slang

Managing your home systems, electrical sockets, and device charges involves unique terminology:

  • 智能家居zi3nang4 gaa1geoi1
    smart home    : Smart home systems.
  • 智能鎖zi3nang4 so2
    smart lock    : A smart lock.
  • 冷氣laang5hei3
    air conditioning / AC    : Air conditioning or AC.
  • 開關hoi1gwaan1
    switch / to turn on or off    : A switch, or to turn on/off.
  • 聲控sing1hung3
    voice-controlled / voice control    : Voice-controlled.
  • 熄sik1
    to turn off / extinguish (a light, device, etc.)    : To turn off a device/light.
  • 燈dang1
    light / lamp    : A light or lamp.
  • 鎖so2
    to lock / lock    : To lock, or a lock itself.
  • 火牛fo2ngau4
    power adapter / charger (slang)    : Literally "fire cow," the popular slang for a power adapter/wall charger.
  • 尿袋niu6doi2
    portable power bank (slang)    : Literally "urine bag," the slang for a portable power bank.
  • 充電cung1din6
    to charge a battery    : To charge a battery.
  • 數據sou3geoi3
    data    : Mobile data.
  • 爆數據baau3 sou3geoi3
    to exceed mobile data limit (slang)    : Exceeding mobile data plans.
  • 密碼mat6maa5
    password    : Password.
  • 戶口wu6hau2
    account (bank/online)    : User account.
  • 就快zau6faai3
    soon / about to    : Soon, about to.
  • 百分之十baak3fan6zi1sap6
    ten percent    : Ten percent.
  • 插頭caap3tau4
    electrical socket / plug    : Electrical socket or plug.
  • 牆coeng4
    wall    : Wall.
  • 系統hai6tung2
    system    : System.
  • 大聲daai6sing1
    loudly / loud voice    : Loudly.

3. Exercises

Test your understanding of smart home terminology in Cantonese.

Practice Exercise

Which of the following refers to a portable power bank in Hong Kong slang?
A) 火牛fo2ngau4
power adapter
B) 智能鎖zi3nang4 so2
smart lock
C) 尿袋niu6doi2
power bank
D) 系統hai6tung2
system

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: C

尿袋niu6doi2
power bank is the local slang for a portable power bank, while 火牛fo2ngau4
power adapter is a power adapter.

Practice Exercise

What is the slang term 火牛fo2ngau4
power adapter literally translated as?
A) Portable power bank
B) Fire cow
C) Smart lock

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: B

火牛fo2ngau4
power adapter literally translates to "fire cow" and refers to a wall power adapter.
