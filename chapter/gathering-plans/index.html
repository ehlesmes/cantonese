Chapter 43

Gathering Plans & Social Slang

Master colloquial Cantonese for organizing gatherings, splitting bills, flaking, and hanging out with friends.

In this chapter, we will learn about Gathering Plans & Social Slang.

Making plans and managing social gatherings comes with its own local slang in Hong Kong.

Let's dive in!

1. Gathering Plans & Shorthand

Here is the essential vocabulary for organizing and describing social events:

  • 局guk6
    gathering / social event / plan    : A gathering, social plan, or get-together (e.g., a drinking or dinner plan).
  • 直落zik6lok6
    to continue onto another venue / next round without stopping    : To continue to another venue or the next round of a night out without stopping.
  • 下場haa6coeng4
    next venue / afterparty / next round    : The next venue, round, or afterparty of the night (literally "next scene").
  • 放飛機fong3fei1gei1
    to stand someone up / break a promise    : To stand someone up, cancel plans last minute, or fail to show up.
  • 飛機王fei1gei1wong4
    chronic flake / person who always stands others up    : A chronic flake; someone who habitually stands people up.
  • 派對paai3deoi3
    party    : A party or festive social gathering.
  • 吹水ceoi1seoi2
    to chat / shoot the breeze / gossip    : To chat, shoot the breeze, or gossip with friends.
  • 潛水cim4seoi2
    to go offline / go silent    : To go offline, disappear from chat groups, or go silent (literally "to dive/snorkel").
  • 得閒dak1haan4
    free / having spare time    : Free, having spare time.
  • AA制ei1ei1zai3
    going Dutch / splitting the bill    : Going Dutch or splitting the bill evenly among the group.

3. Exercises

Test your understanding of gathering terms and flaking slang in Cantonese.

Practice Exercise

Which of the following refers to a person who habitually stands people up or cancels plans last minute?
A) 飛機王fei1gei1wong4
chronic flake
B) 派對paai3deoi3
party
C) 吹水ceoi1seoi2
to chat

Reveal Answer & Explanation
Answer: A

飛機王fei1gei1wong4
chronic flake comes from the phrase 放飛機fong3fei1gei1
to stand someone up and describes a chronic flake.
